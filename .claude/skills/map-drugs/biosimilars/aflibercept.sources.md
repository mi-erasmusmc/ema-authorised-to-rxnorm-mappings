# Aflibercept biosimilar reference

Reviewed 2026-11-11. The TSV covers the twelve authorised EU biosimilar brands in the local EMA medicines report and the six FDA-approved biosimilar families. Empty FDA fields mean no established FDA counterpart was found; they do not mean that the EU product lacks authorisation.

- [FDA biosimilar product information](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/biosimilars/biosimilar-product-information) confirms Yesafili/jbvf (2024-05), Opuviz/yszy (2024-05), Ahzantive/mrbb (2024-06), Enzeevu/abzv (2024-08), Pavblu/ayyh (2024-08), and Eydenzelt/boav (2025-10).
- [Formycon's EU launch announcement](https://www.formycon.com/en/blog/press-release/formycon-expands-commercial-portfolio-aflibercept-biosimilars-ahzantive-and-baiama-are-now-available-in-the-european-union/) identifies Ahzantive and Baiama as FYB203. The [FDA assessment](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2025/761378Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf) identifies Ahzantive as FYB203. Baiama therefore belongs to the aflibercept-mrbb family; its EU brand is not Ahzantive.
- [TGA assessment: Afqlir; Enzeevu](https://www.tga.gov.au/resources/auspar/afqlir-enzeevu) confirms the two Sandoz names within one biosimilar submission. The [FDA Purple Book](https://purplebooksearch.fda.gov/index.cfm?blaNo=761382&event=productdetails) identifies Enzeevu as aflibercept-abzv.
- [EMA Eyluxvi](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/eyluxvi) confirms authorisation on 15 September 2025 and the holder Biolitec pharma Limited Zweigniederlassung Jena. Added this previously missing EU brand.
- [EMA Afiveg assessment](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/afiveg-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf) identifies AVT06; Afiveg and Mynzepli remain without an established FDA-suffixed counterpart. Eiyzey, Vgenfli and Eyluxvi also retain empty FDA fields. Do not assign another biosimilar's suffix based on the shared INN.
- [EMA Skojoy](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/skojoy) states that this duplicate of Pavblu was withdrawn before EU authorisation. It is excluded from the authorised-brand reference and has no local presentation mappings.

Holder spelling follows local regulatory metadata, with normal capitalisation and the corrupted duplicated Polpharma text normalised to Zaklady Farmaceutyczne Polpharma S.A.

Mapping guidance from this review:

- Keep matching branded standard RxNorm concepts. Never substitute Ahzantive for Baiama or Enzeevu for Afqlir.
- Baiama vial: unbranded `0.05 ML aflibercept-mrbb 40 MG/ML Injection`, EXACT. Baiama and Ahzantive syringes: corresponding mrbb Injection, BROAD, with a suffix-specific Prefilled Syringe suggestion; no matching syringe concept was found.
- Yesafili/jbvf and Afqlir/abzv are established regulatory counterparts, but no corresponding RxNorm or Extension concepts were found. Retain the dose-specific plain aflibercept presentation as BROAD and suggest the correct suffix.
- Afiveg, Mynzepli, Eiyzey, Vgenfli and Eyluxvi: plain aflibercept, BROAD, with brand-specific `aflibercept-xxxx` suggestions.
- Opuviz vial: matching branded yszy Injection, EXACT. Opuviz syringe: matching branded yszy Injection, BROAD, with a Prefilled Syringe suggestion.
- Intravitreal adult doses are 2 mg/0.05 mL for the 40 MG/ML family and 8 mg/0.07 mL for high-dose Eylea. Excess fill is expelled; do not substitute fill volumes for delivered doses. Preserve the distinction between Injection and Prefilled Syringe.

Presentation evidence: [Afqlir](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/afqlir-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Yesafili](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/yesafili-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Afiveg](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/afiveg-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf), [Mynzepli](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mynzepli-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Eiyzey](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/eiyzey-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Vgenfli](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/vgenfli-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Eyluxvi](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/eyluxvi-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Eylea](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/eylea-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Pavblu](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/pavblu-epar-product-information_en.pdf), [Opuviz](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/opuviz-epar-product-information_en.pdf), and [Eydenzelt vial SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/101099/smpc). The existing Spain sources file also links CIMA instructions for Baiama, Opuviz and Eydenzelt.

Vocabulary searches used the repository's Hecate `find_concepts` tool, with FDA suffixes, EU brands, strength/form queries and Extension retries. No matching jbvf/abzv presentation or mrbb/yszy syringe was returned. No dose-specific high-dose Eylea Prefilled Syringe was returned; a fill-volume Injection or multi-use Injectable Solution is not an exact substitute.
