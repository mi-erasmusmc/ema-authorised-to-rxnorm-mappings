Rituximab reference review — 2026-11-11

The TSV contains the six authorised EU biosimilar brands, two withdrawn EU duplicates, and the US-only Riabni. Blank FDA fields for Rixathon and Riximyo mean no established FDA equivalent was identified; they must not be assigned another manufacturer's suffix. Dates are FDA approval months, including when the corresponding EU duplicate has a different authorisation date.

- The [FDA biosimilar product list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/biosimilars/biosimilar-product-information) confirms four US brands: Truxima / rituximab-abbs (November 2018), Ruxience / rituximab-pvvr (July 2019), Riabni / rituximab-arrx (December 2020), and Zimrixby / rituximab-cdxx (July 2026). The [Zimrixby approval letter, BLA 761376](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2026/761376Orig1s000ltr.pdf) confirms the proper name and 100 mg/10 mL and 500 mg/50 mL single-dose vials.
- EMA's [Ritemvia withdrawal statement](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/public-statement/public-statement-ritemvia-withdrawal-marketing-authorisation-european-union_en.pdf) explicitly identifies Ritemvia and Blitzima as duplicates of Truxima. Its [Rituzena withdrawal statement](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/public-statement/public-statement-rituzena-withdrawal-marketing-authorisation-european-union_en.pdf) establishes the same for Rituzena, previously Tuxella. Ritemvia was withdrawn in June 2021 and Rituzena in April 2019; both remain in the reference to support historical mappings. All three use the Truxima family, rituximab-abbs, without applying the different Truxima brand to their mappings.
- The Ituxredi / Zimrixby equivalence is established through the shared development code DRL_RI: the [EMA Ituxredi assessment](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/ituxredi-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf) identifies DRL_RI, as does the [FDA October 2025 complete-response letter for BLA 761376](https://download.open.fda.gov/crl/CRL_BLA761376_20251016.pdf). The subsequent approval letter for that same BLA identifies Zimrixby / rituximab-cdxx. The manufacturer's [July 2024 announcement filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1135951/000157587224000835/drr0649_ex99-1.htm) also explicitly names Ituxredi / DRL_RI. This is a product-family link, not an inference from the shared manufacturer alone.
- EU names and holders were checked against the repository's EMA medicines report and EMA pages for [Truxima](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/truxima), [Blitzima](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/blitzima), [Ruxience](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/ruxience), and [Rixathon](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/rixathon), together with local Ituxredi and Riximyo metadata. Rituximab Mabion was an application withdrawn before authorisation and is excluded.

RxNorm searches on the review date found the established abbs, pvvr and arrx families, but no matching cdxx or Zimrixby concept in standard RxNorm or Extension. FDA authorisation therefore does not yet make the Ituxredi mappings EXACT. Use BROAD plain rituximab with a rituximab-cdxx suggestion until matching concepts become available. Rixathon and Riximyo use rituximab-xxxx suggestions; do not use the nonstandard phrase "rituximab biosimilar" as an ingredient.
