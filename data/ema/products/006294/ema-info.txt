Name of medicine: Pomalidomide Zentiva
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: pomalidomide
Active substance: Pomalidomide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Multiple Myeloma
ATC code (human): L04AX06
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of adults with multiple myeloma who have received at least one prior treatment regimen including lenalidomide. Pomalidomide Zentiva in combination with dexamethasone is indicated in the treatment of adult patients with relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma who have received at least two prior treatment regimens, including both lenalidomide and bortezomib, and have demonstrated disease progression on the last therapy.
