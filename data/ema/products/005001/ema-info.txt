EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005001
Name of medicine: Yuvanci
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: macitentan;tadalafil
Active substance: macitentan;tadalafil
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension
ATC code (human): C02KX54
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antihypertensives
Therapeutic indication: Yuvanci is indicated as substitution therapy for the long-term treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in adult patients of WHO Functional Class (FC) II to III, who are already treated with the combination of macitentan and tadalafil given concurrently as separate tablets.
