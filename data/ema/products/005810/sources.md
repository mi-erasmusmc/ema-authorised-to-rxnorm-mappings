# Dyrupeg

[EMA Dyrupeg overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/overview/dyrupeg-epar-medicine-overview_en.pdf) identifies the authorised product EMEA/H/C/006407 as a pegfilgrastim biosimilar to Neulasta. The local presentation EU/1/25/1914/001 is 6 mg in 0.6 mL, matching the generic 0.6 mL pegfilgrastim 10 mg/mL prefilled-syringe concept.

The pegfilgrastim biosimilar reference table and [FDA biosimilar product list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/biosimilars/biosimilar-product-information) do not identify a Dyrupeg FDA-suffixed equivalent. Map BROAD to plain pegfilgrastim and suggest a distinct biosimilar concept, rather than assign another manufacturer's suffix or Neulasta brand.
