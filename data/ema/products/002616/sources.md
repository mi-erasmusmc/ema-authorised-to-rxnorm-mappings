Reviewed 2026-11-06.

- [EMA Wakix product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/wakix-epar-product-information_en.pdf) and [EU authorised Wakix labelling](https://ec.europa.eu/health/documents/community-register/2017/20170914138907/anx_138907_en.pdf): labelled 4.5 mg and 18 mg tablets contain 4.45 mg and 17.8 mg pitolisant, corresponding to 5 mg and 20 mg hydrochloride.
- [EMA Ozawade product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/ozawade-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2: labelled 4.5 mg and 18 mg tablets contain pitolisant hydrochloride equivalent to 4.45 mg and 17.8 mg pitolisant. EU label strengths are rounded base strengths, not salt masses.
- [US Wakix label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=8daa5562-824e-476c-9652-26ceef3d4b0e), section 3: confirms the same base/salt quantities.
- Hecate standard RxNorm searches confirmed both branded Wakix tablet concepts and unbranded concepts used for Ozawade. Each source presentation was assigned by its actual strength.
