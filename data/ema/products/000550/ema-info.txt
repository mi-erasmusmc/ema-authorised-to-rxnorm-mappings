EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000550
Name of medicine: Fendrix
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: hepatitis B (rDNA) vaccine (adjuvanted, adsorbed)
Active substance: hepatitis B surface antigen
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hepatitis B;Immunization
ATC code (human): J07BC01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Vaccines
Therapeutic indication: Fendrix is indicated in adolescents and adults from the age of 15 years onwards for active immunisation against hepatitis B virus infection (HBV) caused by all known subtypes for patients with renal insufficiency (including pre-haemodialysis and haemodialysis patients).
