EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005886
Name of medicine: Spexotras
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: trametinib
Active substance: trametinib dimethyl sulfoxide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Glioma
ATC code (human): L01EE01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Low-grade glioma Spexotras in combination with dabrafenib is indicated for the treatment of paediatric patients aged 1&nbsp;year and older with low-grade glioma (LGG) with a BRAF V600E mutation who require systemic therapy. High-grade glioma Spexotras in combination with dabrafenib is indicated for the treatment of paediatric patients aged 1&nbsp;year and older with high-grade glioma (HGG) with a BRAF V600E mutation who have received at least one prior radiation and/or chemotherapy treatment.
