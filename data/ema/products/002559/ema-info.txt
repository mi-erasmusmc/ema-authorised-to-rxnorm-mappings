EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002559
Name of medicine: Cholib
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: fenofibrate;simvastatin
Active substance: fenofibrate;simvastatin
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Dyslipidemias
ATC code (human): C10BA04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Lipid modifying agents
Therapeutic indication: Cholib is indicated as adjunctive therapy to diet and exercise in high cardiovascular risk adult patients with mixed dyslipidaemia to reduce triglycerides and increase HDL C levels when LDL C levels are adequately controlled with the corresponding dose of simvastatin monotherapy.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
