EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003986
Name of medicine: Praxbind
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: idarucizumab
Active substance: idarucizumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hemorrhage
ATC code (human): V03AB
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): All other therapeutic products
Therapeutic indication: Praxbind is a specific reversal agent for dabigatran and is indicated in adult patients treated with Pradaxa (dabigatran etexilate) when rapid reversal of its anticoagulant effects is required:  for emergency surgery/urgent procedures; in life-threatening or uncontrolled bleeding.
Biosimilar: No
