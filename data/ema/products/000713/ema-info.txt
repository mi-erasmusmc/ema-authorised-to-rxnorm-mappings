EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000713
Name of medicine: Sebivo
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: telbivudine
Active substance: telbivudine
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hepatitis B, Chronic
ATC code (human): J05AF11
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Nucleoside and nucleotide reverse transcriptase inhibitors
Therapeutic indication: Sebivo is indicated for the treatment of chronic hepatitis B in adult patients with compensated liver disease and evidence of viral replication, persistently elevated serum alanine aminotransferase (ALT) levels and histological evidence of active inflammation and/or fibrosis. Initiation of Sebivo treatment should only be considered when the use of an alternative antiviral agent with a higher genetic barrier to resistance is not available or appropriate.
Biosimilar: No
