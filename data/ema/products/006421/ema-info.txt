EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006421
Name of medicine: Rezurock
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: belumosudil
Active substance: belumosudil mesilate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Graft vs Host Disease
ATC code (human): L04AA48
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Rezurock is indicated for the treatment of adults and paediatric patients (12 years and older with a body weight of at least 40 kg) with chronic graft-versus-host disease (cGVHD) when other treatment options provide limited clinical benefit, are not suitable, or have been exhausted.&nbsp;
