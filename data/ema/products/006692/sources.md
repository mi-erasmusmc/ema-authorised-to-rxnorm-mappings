# Aujemflu

The local authorised-presentations PDF and footnote specify a 1 mL prefilled syringe containing 45 micrograms haemagglutinin from each of A/Wisconsin/67/2022 (H1N1)-like, A/District of Columbia/27/2023 (H3N2)-like and B/Austria/1359417/2021-like strains, propagated in MDCK cells, with MF59C.1 adjuvant. Each antigen concentration is 0.045 mg/mL. This is a cell-cultured, adjuvanted trivalent vaccine.

The November 2026 presentations use authorisation identifiers EU/1/26/2050/001 through /004. The mapping uses these identifiers and retains the concept for each corresponding syringe configuration and pack size. [EMA authorised presentations](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/all-authorised-presentations/aujemflu-epar-all-authorised-presentations_en.pdf)

Standard and Extension searches found no complete matching vaccine concept. The generic trivalent Prefilled Syringe concept preserves the three antigen subtypes and presentation form but omits strain detail, 1 mL volume, 45 micrograms HA per strain, MDCK-cell production and MF59C.1 adjuvant. Map BROAD; suggestion preserves strains, antigen concentrations and single-dose volume.
