# Aujemflu

The local authorised-presentations PDF and footnote specify a 1 mL prefilled syringe containing 45 micrograms haemagglutinin from each of A/Wisconsin/67/2022 (H1N1)-like, A/District of Columbia/27/2023 (H3N2)-like and B/Austria/1359417/2021-like strains, propagated in MDCK cells, with MF59C.1 adjuvant. Each antigen concentration is 0.045 mg/mL. This is a cell-cultured, adjuvanted trivalent vaccine.

The local PDF uses provisional EMEA/H/C/006692/001 through /004 identifiers; parsed source IDs are EMA/H/C/006692/001 through /004. Mapping preserves the parsed IDs for source alignment. Actual authorisation identifiers are EU/1/26/2050/001 through /004, as confirmed by [Slovak regulator](https://www.sukl.sk/aujemflu-injekcna-suspenzia-v-naplnenej-injekcnej-striekacke-4955f) and [Lithuanian regulator](https://vapris.vvkt.lt/vvkt-web/public/medications/view/37131). A future source-refresh should reconcile these identifiers.
