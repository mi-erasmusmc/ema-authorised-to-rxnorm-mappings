# Adstiladrin

- [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/adstiladrin-epar-product-information_en.pdf) and the local authorised-presentations PDF specify 3 × 10^11 viral particles/mL, in 20 mL single-dose vials, four vials per carton.
- [US prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5ca20ed4-da4f-463c-ac2a-ccd6ab3774fa), sections 2.1, 3 and 11, also specifies the same nominal concentration, with a 75 mL treatment dose and at least 20 mL extractable per vial.
- Standard RxNorm and Extension searches returned strength-specific concepts at 15000000000 VIRAL-PARTICLES/ML, which do not match the labels. No correctly concentrated concept was found. Use the branded intravesical-suspension form concept BROAD and preserve the labelled concentration and 20 mL single-use vial in the suggestion.
