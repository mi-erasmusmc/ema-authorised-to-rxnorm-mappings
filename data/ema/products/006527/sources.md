# Susvimo

[EMA assessment report](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/susvimo-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf) explicitly distinguishes the 0.395 mL target vial fill from the 0.1 mL nominal label claim (10 mg). The nominal volume ensures delivery of the 2 mg implant dose. Map EXACT to the standard 0.1 mL ranibizumab 100 mg/mL Injection [Susvimo] concept; source 0.395 mL includes manufacturing fill overage rather than a different nominal presentation.
