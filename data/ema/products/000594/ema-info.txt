EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000594
Name of medicine: Truvada
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: emtricitabine;tenofovir disoproxil
Active substance: emtricitabine;tenofovir disoproxil fumarate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): HIV Infections
ATC code (human): J05AR03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antivirals for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of HIV-1 infection:  Truvada is indicated in antiretroviral combination therapy for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adults. Truvada is also indicated for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adolescents, with NRTI resistance or toxicities precluding the use of first line agents, aged 12 to &lt; 18 years.  Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP):  Truvada is indicated in combination with safer sex practices for pre-exposure prophylaxis to reduce the risk of sexually acquired HIV-1 infection in adults at high risk.
