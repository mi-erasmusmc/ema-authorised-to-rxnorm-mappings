EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005863
Name of medicine: Jaypirca
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: pirtobrutinib
Active substance: pirtobrutinib
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Lymphoma, Mantle-Cell
ATC code (human): L01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents;Protein kinase inhibitors
Therapeutic indication: Jaypirca as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory mantle cell lymphoma (MCL) who have been previously treated with a Bruton’s tyrosine kinase (BTK) inhibitor. Jaypirca as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed or refractory chronic lymphocytic leukaemia (CLL) who have been previously treated with a BTK inhibitor.
