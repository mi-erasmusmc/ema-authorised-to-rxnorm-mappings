EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005167
Name of medicine: Dovprela (previously Pretomanid FGK)
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: pretomanid
Active substance: pretomanid
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Tuberculosis, Multidrug-Resistant
ATC code (human): J04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antimycobacterials
Therapeutic indication: Dovprela is indicated in combination with bedaquiline and linezolid, in adults, for the treatment of pulmonary extensively drug resistant (XDR), or treatment-intolerant or nonresponsive multidrug-resistant (MDR) tuberculosis (TB). Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
