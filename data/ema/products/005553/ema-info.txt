EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005553
Name of medicine: Aspaveli
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: pegcetacoplan
Active substance: pegcetacoplan
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hemoglobinuria, Paroxysmal
ATC code (human): L04AJ03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Aspaveli is indicated as monotherapy in the treatment of adult patients with paroxysmal nocturnal haemoglobinuria (PNH) who have haemolytic anaemia. Aspaveli is indicated for the treatment of adult and adolescent patients aged 12 to 17 years with C3 glomerulopathy (C3G) or primary immune-complex membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis (IC-MPGN) in combination with a renin-angiotensin system (RAS) inhibitor, unless RAS inhibitor treatment is not tolerated or contraindicated.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
