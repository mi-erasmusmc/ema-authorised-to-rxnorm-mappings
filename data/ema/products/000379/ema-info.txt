EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000379
Name of medicine: Cancidas (previously Caspofungin MSD)
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: caspofungin
Active substance: caspofungin (as acetate)
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Candidiasis;Aspergillosis
ATC code (human): J02AX04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antimycotics for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of invasive candidiasis in adult or paediatric patients; treatment of invasive aspergillosis in adult or paediatric patients who are refractory to or intolerant of amphotericin B, lipid formulations of amphotericin B and / or itraconazole. Refractoriness is defined as progression of infection or failure to improve after a minimum of seven days of prior therapeutic doses of effective antifungal therapy; empirical therapy for presumed fungal infections (such as Candida or Aspergillus) in febrile, neutropaenic adult or paediatric patients.
Biosimilar: No
