EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000996
Name of medicine: Cayston
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: aztreonam
Active substance: aztreonam lysine
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Cystic Fibrosis;Respiratory Tract Infections
ATC code (human): J01DF01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antibacterials for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Cayston is indicated for the suppressive therapy of chronic pulmonary infections due to Pseudomonas aeruginosa in patients with cystic fibrosis (CF) aged 6 years and older. Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
