# Vanflyta

- [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/vanflyta-epar-product-information_en.pdf) states quizartinib content as free-base equivalents supplied as dihydrochloride.
- [US label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=29cdbcfe-497d-4e78-bb7b-2d4acafe8e86): 20 mg and 30 mg dihydrochloride correspond to the same 17.7 mg and 26.5 mg quizartinib tablet strengths.
