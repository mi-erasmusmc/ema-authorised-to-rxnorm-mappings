EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002314
Name of medicine: Duavive
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: oestrogens conjugated;bazedoxifene
Active substance: oestrogens conjugated;bazedoxifene
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Postmenopause
ATC code (human): G03CC07
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): conjugated estrogens and bazedoxifene
Therapeutic indication: Duavive is indicated for:  Treatment of oestrogen deficiency symptoms in postmenopausal women with a uterus (with at least 12 months since the last menses) for whom treatment with progestin-containing therapy is not appropriate.  The experience treating women older than 65 years is limited.
