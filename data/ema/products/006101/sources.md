# Uzpruvo mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-11.

[Alvotech business update](https://www.alvotech.com/newsroom/alvotech-reports-results-for-the-first-nine-months-of-2025-and-provides-a-business-update) identifies AVT04 as Uzpruvo in the EEA and Selarsdi (ustekinumab-aekn) in the US. The local ustekinumab biosimilar reference table records the same equivalence.

[FDA Selarsdi label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/761343Orig1s005lbl.pdf) confirms the 45 mg/0.5 mL single-dose vial. [DailyMed Selarsdi prescribing information](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b3b483f4-3426-4d6d-97ae-39848ec1bfae) describes the subcutaneous and intravenous presentations.

Standard RxNorm searches verified all presentation families: 0.5 ML ustekinumab-aekn 90 MG/ML Prefilled Syringe (1466414; RxCUI 2705393), 1 ML ustekinumab-aekn 90 MG/ML Prefilled Syringe (1466416; RxCUI 2705400), 0.5 ML ustekinumab-aekn 90 MG/ML Injection (37355206; RxCUI 2726116), and 26 ML ustekinumab-aekn 5 MG/ML Injection (1466412; RxCUI 2705382).

The 45 mg vial row EU/1/23/1784/003 was corrected from a plain-ustekinumab BROAD mapping to the matching aekn EXACT concept. The other four EXACT mappings were verified against the individual source strength, volume and device and retained. Unbranded concepts are used because the EU brand Uzpruvo differs from the US brand Selarsdi. All five rows are EXACT.
