Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs for obstructive airway diseases
Therapeutic indication: Asthma&nbsp;Exdensur is indicated as add-on maintenance treatment for severe asthma with type 2 inflammation characterised by blood eosinophil count in adults and adolescents 12 years and older who are inadequately controlled&nbsp;despite high dose inhaled corticosteroids (ICS) plus another asthma controller (see section 5.1). Chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP)&nbsp;Exdensur is indicated as an add-on therapy with intranasal corticosteroids for the treatment of adult patients with severe CRSwNP for whom therapy with systemic corticosteroids and/or surgery do not provide adequate disease control.&nbsp;
