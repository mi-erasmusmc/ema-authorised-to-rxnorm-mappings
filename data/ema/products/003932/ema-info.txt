EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003932
Name of medicine: Zurampic
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: lesinurad
Active substance: lesinurad
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hyperuricemia
ATC code (human): M04AB05
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antigout preparations
Therapeutic indication: Zurampic, in combination with a xanthine oxidase inhibitor, is indicated in adults for the adjunctive treatment of hyperuricaemia in gout patients (with or without tophi) who have not achieved target serum uric acid levels with an adequate dose of a xanthine oxidase inhibitor alone.
