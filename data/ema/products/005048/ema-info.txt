EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005048
Name of medicine: Xenleta
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: lefamulin
Active substance: lefamulin acetate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Pneumonia, Bacterial;Community-Acquired Infections
ATC code (human): J01XX
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antibacterials for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Xenleta is indicated for the treatment of community-acquired pneumonia (CAP) in adults when it is considered inappropriate to use antibacterial agents that are commonly recommended for the initial treatment of CAP or when these have failed. Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
