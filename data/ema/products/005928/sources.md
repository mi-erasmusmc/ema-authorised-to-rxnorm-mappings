# Eladynos mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-11.

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/eladynos-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 6.5, specifies 3 mg abaloparatide in 1.5 mL (2 mg/mL), with 30 delivered doses per pre-filled pen. Each dose is 80 micrograms in 0.04 mL. The [Spanish regulator product information](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1221706001/FT_1221706001.html) provides the same authorised presentation.

[EMA public assessment report](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/eladynos-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf), pharmaceutical development, explicitly describes 0.3 mL overfill for priming. The 30 delivered doses consume 1.2 mL.

[US Tymlos labelling](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=712143d9-e21e-4013-bb3b-3426a21060a8), section 3, specifies a 1.56 mL pen at 2 mg/mL delivering the same 30 doses of 80 micrograms in 0.04 mL.

Mapping decision: both EMA presentations map EXACT to standard unbranded concept 1594152, 1.56 ML abaloparatide 2 MG/ML Pen Injector (RxCUI 1921075). This is a clinical-equivalence judgment based on matching ingredient, concentration, device class, delivered dose and usable dose count. The 1.5 versus 1.56 mL labelled fill difference is documented in each mapping comment. The sources establish matching delivered-dose presentations; they do not establish identical physical fill volumes or prove that the entire between-label difference is attributable to overfill.

The different US brand Tymlos is excluded. Extension searches found 1.5 mL Injectable Solution concepts, but these omit the pen device.

Validation retains the pre-existing NO_DATE issue because parsed_data.tsv has no date suffix and its parsing date is undocumented.
