Therapeutic area (MeSH): Anthrax

Therapeutic indication: Obiltoxaximab SFL is indicated in combination with appropriate antibacterial drugs in all age groups for treatment of inhalational anthrax due to Bacillus anthracis (see section 5.1). Obiltoxaximab SFL is indicated in all age groups for post-exposure prophylaxis of inhalational anthrax when alternative therapies are not appropriate or are not available (see section 5.1).
