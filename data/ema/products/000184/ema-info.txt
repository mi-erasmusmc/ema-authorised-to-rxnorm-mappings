EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000184
Name of medicine: Evista
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: raloxifene
Active substance: raloxifene hydrochloride
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Osteoporosis, Postmenopausal
ATC code (human): G03XC01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Sex hormones and modulators of the genital system
Therapeutic indication: Evista is indicated for the treatment and prevention of osteoporosis in post-menopausal women. A significant reduction in the incidence of vertebral, but not hip fractures has been demonstrated. When determining the choice of Evista or other therapies, including oestrogens, for an individual postmenopausal woman, consideration should be given to menopausal symptoms, effects on uterine and breast tissues, and cardiovascular risks and benefits.
