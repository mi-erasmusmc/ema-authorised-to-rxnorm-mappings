
Mapping review 2026-11-06.

[EMA variation opinion](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/variation/riltrava-aerosphere). The new strength contains 18 micrograms glycopyrronium bromide equivalent to 14.4 micrograms glycopyrronium, 5 micrograms formoterol fumarate dihydrate and 160 micrograms budesonide per delivered actuation. RxNorm glycopyrrolate is the bromide form and formoterol fumarate is anhydrous (4.8 micrograms), matching the new standard 120-actuation 160/4.8/18 concept. The older lower-strength rows are unchanged.

The [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/riltrava-aerosphere-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2, confirms the lower-strength delivered dose: 9 micrograms glycopyrronium bromide equivalent to 7.2 micrograms glycopyrronium. Corrected the older two mapping comments that incorrectly called the RxNorm 9-microgram amount base; concepts and EXACT types are retained.
