EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000425
Name of medicine: Forsteo
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: teriparatide
Active substance: teriparatide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Osteoporosis;Osteoporosis, Postmenopausal
ATC code (human): H05AA02
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Calcium homeostasis
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women and in men at increased risk of fracture. In postmenopausal women, a significant reduction in the incidence of vertebral and nonvertebral fractures but not hip fractures has been demonstrated. Treatment of osteoporosis associated with sustained systemic glucocorticoid therapy in women and men at increased risk for fracture.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
