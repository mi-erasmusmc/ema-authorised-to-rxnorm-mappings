EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000839
Name of medicine: Volibris
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ambrisentan
Active substance: ambrisentan
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypertension, Pulmonary
ATC code (human): C02KX02
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antihypertensives
Therapeutic indication: Volibris is indicated for treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in adult patients of WHO Functional Class (FC) II to III, including use in combination treatment (see section 5.1).  Efficacy has been shown in idiopathic PAH (IPAH) and in PAH associated with connective tissue disease. Volibris is indicated for treatment of PAH in adolescents and children (aged 8 to less than 18 years) of WHO Functional Class (FC) II to III including use in combination treatment. Efficacy has been shown in IPAH, familial, corrected congenital and in PAH associated with connective tissue disease (see section 5.1).
