# Sialanar mapping evidence — 2026-11-06

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/sialanar-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2–3: solution 0.32 mg/mL glycopyrronium equals 0.4 mg/mL bromide. Tablets contain 0.68/1.36 mg glycopyrronium, equivalent to 0.85/1.7 mg bromide, and are orodispersible.

[DailyMed Dartisla ODT label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=52daf47e-7ba3-4105-be7f-27001a87e16e), Description and product information: US glycopyrrolate is the bromide salt; tablet strengths are salt-based. Use unbranded standard disintegrating-tablet concepts after conversion.

Hecate standard searches found no matching oral-solution concentration. Extension supplies clean 0.32 mg/mL glycopyrronium Oral Solution [Sialanar] (1235552 / OMOP5216259), adding clinically relevant strength. Rejected 0.3 and 0.4 mg/mL glycopyrronium-labelled Extension variants because those do not match the labelled base concentration. Multidose bottle size does not change concentration-level mapping.
