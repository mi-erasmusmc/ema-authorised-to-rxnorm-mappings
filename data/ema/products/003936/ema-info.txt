EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003936
Name of medicine: Arikayce
Medicine status: Application withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: 
Active substance: amikacin sulfate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Mycobacterium Infections
ATC code (human): J01GB06
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antibacterials for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Arikayce is indicated for:  treatment of Mycobacterium avium Complex (MAC) lung disease in adult patients who have persistent positive sputum cultures despite the use of medically appropriate first-line therapy  Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents. Arikayce should be added to existing therapy and should not be used as monotherapy.
