EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002735
Name of medicine: Eperzan
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: albiglutide
Active substance: albiglutide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Diabetes Mellitus, Type 2
ATC code (human): A10BJ04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs used in diabetes
Therapeutic indication: Eperzan is indicated for the treatment of type 2 diabetes mellitus in adults to improve glycaemic control as: Monotherapy When diet and exercise alone do not provide adequate glycaemic control in patients for whom use of metformin is considered inappropriate due to contraindications or intolerance. Add-on combination therapy In combination with other glucose-lowering medicinal products including basal insulin, when these, together with diet and exercise, do not provide adequate glycaemic control (see section 4.4 and 5.1 for available data on different combinations).
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
