The [EMA Docetaxel Mylan product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/docetaxel-mylan-epar-product-information_en.pdf), preparation instructions, confirms 20 mg/mL concentrate and single-use vials. The local authorised presentations contain 20 mg/1 mL, 80 mg/4 mL, or 200 mg/10 mL, all at 20 MG/ML. Standard RxNorm supplies the corresponding unbranded volume-specific Injection concepts; map the supplied concentrate before infusion dilution.
