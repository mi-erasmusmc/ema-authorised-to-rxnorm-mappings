EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006061
Name of medicine: Piasky
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: crovalimab
Active substance: 
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hemoglobinuria, Paroxysmal
ATC code (human): L04AJ07
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants, Complement inhibitors
Therapeutic indication: Piasky as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult and paediatric patients 12 years of age or older with a weight of 40 kg and above with paroxysmal nocturnal haemoglobinuria (PNH):  In patients with haemolysis with clinical symptom(s) indicative of high disease activity. In patients who are clinically stable after having been treated with a complement component 5 (C5) inhibitor for at least the past 6 months.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
