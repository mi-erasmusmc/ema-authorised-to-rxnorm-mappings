EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/001131
Name of medicine: Arzerra
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ofatumumab
Active substance: ofatumumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Leukemia, Lymphocytic, Chronic, B-Cell
ATC code (human): L01XC10
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Monoclonal antibodies
Therapeutic indication: Previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukaemia (CLL): Arzerra in combination with chlorambucil or bendamustine is indicated for the treatment of patients with CLL who have not received prior therapy and who are not eligible for fludarabine-based therapy. Relapsed CLL: Arzerra is indicated in combination with fludarabine and cyclophosphamide for the treatment of adult patients with relapsed CLL. Refractory CLL: Arzerra is indicated for the treatment of CLL in patients who are refractory to fludarabine and alemtuzumab.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
