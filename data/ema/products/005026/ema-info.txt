EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005026
Name of medicine: Inrebic
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: fedratinib
Active substance: fedratinib dihydrochloride monohydrate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Myeloproliferative Disorders;Primary Myelofibrosis
ATC code (human): L01EJ02
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Inrebic is indicated for the treatment of disease-related splenomegaly or symptoms in adult patients with primary myelofibrosis, post polycythaemia vera myelofibrosis or post essential thrombocythaemia myelofibrosis who are Janus Associated Kinase (JAK) inhibitor naïve or have been treated with ruxolitinib.
