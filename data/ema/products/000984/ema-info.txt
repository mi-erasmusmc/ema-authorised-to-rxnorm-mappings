EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000984
Name of medicine: Efient
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: prasugrel
Active substance: prasugrel
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Acute Coronary Syndrome;Angina, Unstable;Myocardial Infarction
ATC code (human): B01AC22
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antithrombotic agents
Therapeutic indication: Efient, co-administered with acetylsalicylic acid (ASA), is indicated for the prevention of atherothrombotic events in patients with acute coronary syndrome (i.e. unstable angina, non-ST-segment-elevation myocardial infarction [UA / NSTEMI] or ST-segment-elevation myocardial infarction [STEMI]) undergoing primary or delayed percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI).
