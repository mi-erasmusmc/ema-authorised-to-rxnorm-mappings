EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004750
Name of medicine: Zolgensma
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: onasemnogene abeparvovec
Active substance: onasemnogene abeparvovec
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Muscular Atrophy, Spinal
ATC code (human): M09AX09
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other drugs for disorders of the musculo-skeletal system
Therapeutic indication: Zolgensma is indicated for the treatment of:  patients with 5q spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) with a bi-allelic mutation in the SMN1 gene and a clinical diagnosis of SMA Type 1, or patients with 5q SMA with a bi-allelic mutation in the SMN1 gene and up to 3 copies of the SMN2 gene.
