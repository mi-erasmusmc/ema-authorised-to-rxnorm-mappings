Name of medicine: Afqlir
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: aflibercept

Therapeutic area (MeSH): Wet Macular Degeneration;Macular Edema;Diabetes Complications;Retinal Vein Occlusion
ATC code (human): S01LA05

Therapeutic indication: Afqlir is indicated for adults for the treatment of • neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (AMD) • visual impairment due to macular oedema secondary to retinal vein occlusion (branch RVO or central RVO) • visual impairment due to diabetic macular oedema (DME) • visual impairment due to myopic choroidal neovascularisation (myopic CNV)
