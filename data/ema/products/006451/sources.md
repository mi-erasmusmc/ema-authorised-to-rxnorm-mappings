# Imdylltra

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/imdylltra-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 6.6, identifies single-use powder vials containing 1 mg or 10 mg tarlatamab. The accompanying 7 mL solution vials are IV solution stabiliser, not additional active ingredient. The 0.9/2.4 mg/mL concentrations refer to reconstituted tarlatamab, not the supplied stabiliser strength. Map the labelled powder amount using Injection.

[US label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=1e7b6163-5d83-42ea-82c9-cf7620cdc782) identifies the same tarlatamab product with the US suffix -dlle in 1 mg and 10 mg single-use powder vials. Use unbranded concepts because the EU spelling Imdylltra differs from US Imdelltra.
