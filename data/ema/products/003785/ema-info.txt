Name of medicine: Tenkasi (previously Orbactiv)
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: oritavancin
Active substance: oritavancin (diphosphate)
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Soft Tissue Infections;Skin Diseases, Bacterial
ATC code (human): J01XA05
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antibacterials for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Tenkasi is indicated for the treatment of acute bacterial skin and skin structure infections (ABSSSI) in adults and paediatric patients aged 3 months and older (see sections 4.2, 4.4 and 5.1). Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
