EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002709
Name of medicine: Granupas (previously Para-aminosalicylic acid Lucane)
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: para-aminosalicylic acid
Active substance: para-aminosalicylic acid
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Tuberculosis
ATC code (human): J04AA01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antimycobacterials
Therapeutic indication: Granupas is indicated for use as part of an appropriate combination regimen for multi-drug resistant tuberculosis in adults and paediatric patients from 28 days of age and older when an effective treatment regimen cannot otherwise be composed for reasons of resistance or tolerability (see section 4.4). Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
