EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003774
Name of medicine: Uptravi
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: selexipag
Active substance: selexipag
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypertension, Pulmonary
ATC code (human): B01AC27
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antithrombotic agents
Therapeutic indication: Uptravi is indicated for the long-term treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in adult patients with WHO functional class (FC) II–III, either as combination therapy in patients insufficiently controlled with an endothelin receptor antagonist (ERA) and/or a phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE-5) inhibitor, or as monotherapy in patients who are not candidates for these therapies. Efficacy has been shown in a PAH population including idiopathic and heritable PAH, PAH associated with connective tissue disorders, and PAH associated with corrected simple congenital heart disease.
