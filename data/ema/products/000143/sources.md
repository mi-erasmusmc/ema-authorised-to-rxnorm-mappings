Liprolog Mix25 contains 25% insulin lispro and 75% insulin lispro protamine; Mix50 contains 50% of each, at 100 units/mL total. See the [EMA Liprolog overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/overview/liprolog-epar-medicine-overview_en.pdf).

RxNorm Extension supplies the matching mixed-insulin cartridge forms absent from standard RxNorm. The remaining presentations use standard RxNorm concepts.
