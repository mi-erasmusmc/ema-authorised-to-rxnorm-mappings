EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000143
Name of medicine: Liprolog
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: insulin lispro
Active substance: insulin lispro
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Diabetes Mellitus
ATC code (human): A10AB04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs used in diabetes;Insulins and analogues for injection, fast-acting
Therapeutic indication: For the treatment of patients with diabetes mellitus who require insulin for the maintenance of normal glucose homeostasis. Liprolog is also indicated for the initial stabilization of diabetes mellitus. Liprolog is a short acting insulin and may be used in conjunction with a longer acting human insulin. Liprolog is indicated for preprandial administration.
