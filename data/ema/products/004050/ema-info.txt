EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004050
Name of medicine: Emtricitabine/Tenofovir disoproxil Mylan
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: emtricitabine;tenofovir disoproxil
Active substance: emtricitabine;tenofovir disoproxil maleate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): HIV Infections
ATC code (human): J05AR03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antivirals for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of HIV-1 infection:  Emtricitabine/Tenofovir disoproxil Mylan is indicated in antiretroviral combination therapy for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adults (see section 5.1). Emtricitabine/Tenofovir disoproxil Mylan is also indicated for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adolescents, with NRTI resistance or toxicities precluding the use of first line agents, (see sections 4.2, 4.4 and 5.1).  Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP):  Emtricitabine/Tenofovir disoproxil Mylan is indicated in combination with safer sex practices for pre-exposure prophylaxis to reduce the risk of sexually acquired HIV-1 infection in adults and adolescents at high risk (see sections 4.2, 4.4 and 5.1).
