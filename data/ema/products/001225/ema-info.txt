EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/001225
Name of medicine: Colobreathe
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: colistimethate sodium
Active substance: Colistimethate sodium
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Cystic Fibrosis
ATC code (human): J01XB01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antibacterials for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Colobreathe is indicated for the management of chronic pulmonary infections due to Pseudomonas aeruginosa in patients with cystic fibrosis (CF) aged six years and older. Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
