International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: aliskiren;amlodipine;hydrochlorothiazide
Active substance: aliskiren;amlodipine;hydrochlorothiazide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypertension
ATC code (human): C09XA54
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Cardiovascular system
Therapeutic indication: Rasitrio is indicated for the treatment of essential hypertension as substitution therapy in adult patients whose blood pressure is adequately controlled on the combination of aliskiren, amlodipine and hydrochlorothiazide given concurrently at the same dose level as in the combination.
