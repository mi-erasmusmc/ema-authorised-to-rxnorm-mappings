# Eladynos

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/eladynos-epar-product-information_en.pdf) confirms 80 micrograms per 40 microlitres, 3 mg in each 1.5 mL pen (2 mg/mL), and 30 doses per pen.

Standard RxNorm has a concentration-level Pen Injector and a 1.56 mL Pen Injector. Extension returned 1.5 mL Injectable Solution concepts, but no 1.5 mL Pen Injector. The concentration-level Pen Injector preserves the device and strength; mapped BROAD with the precise volume in the suggestion.
