# Orladeyo mapping evidence — 2026-11-06

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/orladeyo-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2–3: sachets contain 72/96/108/132 mg berotralstat as dihydrochloride; the formulation consists of rounded film-coated granules approximately 2 mm in diameter.

[US manufacturer prescribing information](https://www.biocryst.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/13/2026/10/ORLADEYO_USPI.pdf) and [DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=3527ece1-e7e0-40b1-b0c5-6baeb0deabeb&type=display) describe the corresponding unit-dose film-coated oral pellets. Hecate standard RxNorm contains all four matching strengths as Oral Pellet [Orladeyo]. Existing 150 mg capsule mappings are retained.
