The [EMA Clopidogrel Qualimed positive opinion](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/smop-initial/committee-medicinal-products-human-use-summary-positive-opinion-clopidogrel-qualimed_en.pdf) identifies 75 mg film-coated tablets containing clopidogrel as hydrochloride. The labelled strength is the clopidogrel amount; use the standard unbranded clopidogrel 75 MG Oral Tablet concept. The opinion identifies ATC B01AC04; the B01AC06 code in local ema-info.txt is inconsistent with that primary source and was not used to choose the ingredient.
