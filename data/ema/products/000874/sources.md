The [EMA DuoPlavin product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/duoplavin-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2, confirms that the strength order is clopidogrel followed by acetylsalicylic acid (aspirin): 75/75 mg or 75/100 mg. RxNorm names list aspirin first. Standard RxNorm searches did not return the complete combination; clean RxNorm Extension Oral Tablet concepts preserve both ingredients and strengths. The 75/75 mg mapping uses the generic concept rather than the available branded concept expressing clopidogrel as 97.9 mg. The 75/100 mg concept also captures the matching DuoPlavin brand.
