Name of medicine: Nevirapine Teva
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: nevirapine
Active substance: nevirapine
Therapeutic area (MeSH): HIV Infections
ATC code (human): J05AG01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antivirals for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Nevirapine Teva is indicated in combination with other anti-retroviral medicinal products for the treatment of HIV 1 infected adults, adolescents, and children of any age. Most of the experience with nevirapine is in combination with nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitors (NRTIs). The choice of a subsequent therapy after nevirapine should be based on clinical experience and resistance testing.
