EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003833
Name of medicine: Glyxambi
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: empagliflozin;linagliptin
Active substance: empagliflozin;linagliptin
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Diabetes Mellitus, Type 2
ATC code (human): A10BD19
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs used in diabetes
Therapeutic indication: Glyxambi, fixed dose combination of empagliflozin and linagliptin, is indicated in adults aged 18 years and older with type 2 diabetes mellitus:  to improve glycaemic control when metformin and/or sulphonylurea (SU) and one of the monocomponents of Glyxambi do not provide adequate glycaemic control; when already being treated with the free combination of empagliflozin and linagliptin.
