EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000306
Name of medicine: Metalyse
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: tenecteplase
Active substance: tenecteplase
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Myocardial Infarction
ATC code (human): B01AD11
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antithrombotic agents
Therapeutic indication: Metalyse is indicated for the thrombolytic treatment of suspected myocardial infarction with persistent ST elevation or recent left-bundle-branch block within six hours after the onset of acute-myocardial-infarction symptoms. Metalyse is indicated in adults for the thrombolytic treatment of acute ischaemic stroke (AIS) within 4.5 hours from last known well and after exclusion of intracranial haemorrhage.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
