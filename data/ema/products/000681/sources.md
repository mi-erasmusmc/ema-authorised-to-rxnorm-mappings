[EMA Voraxaze product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/voraxaze-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 6.6: the powder vial contains nominally 1000 units and is reconstituted with 1 mL sodium chloride solution to 1000 units/mL. The presentation PDF lists the reconstituted concentration. The standard RxNorm 1000 UNT Injection concept captures the powder vial.
