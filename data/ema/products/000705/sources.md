The [EMA Mylotarg product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mylotarg-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 6.6, states that the 5 mg vial is reconstituted with 5 mL to 1 mg/mL, with 4.5 mg (4.5 mL) extractable. The [US manufacturer preparation instructions](https://mylotarg.pfizerpro.com/dosing/relapsed-refractory-aml-single-agent-regimen/preparation-administration) confirm the same 5 mg vial content and 4.5 mg extractable amount for the US-labelled product. Therefore the standard RxNorm 4.5 MG Injection [Mylotarg] concept is an EXACT match; the label-strength difference does not require Extension.
