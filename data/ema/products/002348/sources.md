# DuoResp Spiromax mapping evidence

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/duoresp-spiromax-epar-product-information_en.pdf), reviewed 2026-11-11.

SmPC section 2 confirms delivered doses of 160/4.5 micrograms and 320/9 micrograms, equivalent to metered doses of 200/6 micrograms and 400/12 micrograms respectively. The packaging sections confirm 120 doses per lower-strength inhaler and 60 doses per higher-strength inhaler.

The lower-strength mapping uses the delivered-dose, branded 120-actuation RxNorm Extension dry powder inhaler concept 43043379. The existing higher-strength concept 35421264 already matches the delivered dose and 60-actuation dry powder inhaler and is retained. Standard RxNorm search returned liquid Metered Dose Inhaler concepts, which do not match the powder form.
