Mapping evidence checked 2026-11-06.

- [EMA Sondelbay product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/sondelbay-epar-product-information_en.pdf) identifies Sondelbay as a biosimilar and specifies 28 doses of 20 micrograms per prefilled pen. Each dose occupies 80 microlitres; the cartridge reservoir contains 2.4 mL. Thus the deliverable presentation is represented by 28 ACTUAT at 0.02 MG/ACTUAT, rather than dividing reservoir volume by dose volume to infer 30 doses.
- All biosimilar searches (brand, ingredient with brand/strength/form, unbranded strength/form, ingredient) found no matching FDA-suffixed Sondelbay concept. Standard unbranded teriparatide is therefore BROAD under the biosimilar rule. The standard 28-dose concept preserves the clinical dose and device, so the Extension 2.4 mL concept is not needed.
- The actual presentations are EU/1/22/1628/001-003; the folder metadata concerns an earlier withdrawn application under the same name.
