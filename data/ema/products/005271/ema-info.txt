Name of medicine: Zokinvy
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: lonafarnib
Active substance: Lonafarnib
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Progeria;Laminopathies
ATC code (human): 
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other alimentary tract and metabolism products
Therapeutic indication: Zokinvy is indicated for the treatment of patients 12 months of age and older with a genetically confirmed diagnosis of Hutchinson-Gilford progeria syndrome or a processing-deficient progeroid laminopathy associated with either a heterozygous LMNA mutation with progerin-like protein accumulation or a homozygous or compound heterozygous ZMPSTE24 mutation.
