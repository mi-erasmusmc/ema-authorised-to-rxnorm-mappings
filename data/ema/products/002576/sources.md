Mapping evidence

- [EMA Remsima product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/remsima-epar-product-information_en.pdf): 40 mg/mL SmPC sections 2 and 6.6 describe 100 mg and 350 mg liquid concentrate vials and prohibit storing unused infusion solution for reuse. Injection is the appropriate single-use dose form; Injectable Solution denotes multiple-use under the search dose-form definitions.
- [FDA Zymfentra review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/nda/2024/761358Orig1s000MultidisciplineR.pdf): identifies CT-P13 SC as infliximab-dyyb and its European name as Remsima SC, supporting the existing unbranded infliximab-dyyb syringe and pen mappings.
- [FDA substance registry](https://precision.fda.gov/ginas/app/ui/browse-substance?search=root_codes_comments%3A%22Remsima%22): cross-references Remsima, CT-P13 and infliximab-dyyb.
