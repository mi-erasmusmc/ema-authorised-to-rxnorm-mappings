EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005084
Name of medicine: MenQuadfi
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: meningococcal group A, C, W-135 and Y conjugate vaccine
Active substance: Neisseria meningitidis group C polysaccharide conjugated to tetanus toxoid;Neisseria meningitidis group A polysaccharide conjugated to tetanus toxoid;Neisseria meningitidis group Y polysaccharide conjugated to tetanus toxoid;Neisseria meningitidis group W-135 polysaccharide conjugated to tetanus toxoid
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Meningitis, Meningococcal
ATC code (human): J07AH08
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Vaccines
Therapeutic indication: MenQuadfi is indicated for active immunisation of individuals from the age of 12 months and older against invasive meningococcal disease caused by Neisseria meningitidis serogroups A, C, W, and Y. The use of this vaccine should be in accordance with available official recommendations.
