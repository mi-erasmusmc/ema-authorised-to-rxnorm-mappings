EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/001045
Name of medicine: Scintimun
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: besilesomab
Active substance: besilesomab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Osteomyelitis;Radionuclide Imaging
ATC code (human): V09HA03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Diagnostic radiopharmaceuticals
Therapeutic indication: This medicinal product is for diagnostic use only and the approved indication is scintigraphic imaging, in conjunction with other appropriate imaging modalities, for determining the location of inflammation/infection in peripheral bone in adults with suspected osteomyelitis. Scintimun should not be used for the diagnosis of diabetic foot infection.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
