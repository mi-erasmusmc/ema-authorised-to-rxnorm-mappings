EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002393
Name of medicine: Defitelio
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: defibrotide
Active substance: defibrotide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hepatic Veno-Occlusive Disease
ATC code (human): B01AX01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antithrombotic agents
Therapeutic indication: Defitelio is indicated for the treatment of severe hepatic veno-occlusive disease (VOD) also known as sinusoidal obstructive syndrome (SOS) in haematopoietic stem-cell transplantation (HSCT) therapy. It is indicated in adults and in adolescents, children and infants over 1 month of age.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
