EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004919
Name of medicine: Vitrakvi
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: larotrectinib
Active substance: larotrectinib sulfate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Abdominal Neoplasms
ATC code (human): L01XE53
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Vitrakvi as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult and paediatric patients with solid tumours that display a Neurotrophic Tyrosine Receptor Kinase (NTRK) gene fusion,  who have a disease that is locally advanced, metastatic or where surgical resection is likely to result in severe morbidity, and who have no satisfactory treatment options.
