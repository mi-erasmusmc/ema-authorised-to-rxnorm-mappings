Name of medicine: Daurismo

International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: glasdegib

Therapeutic area (MeSH): Leukemia, Myeloid, Acute

Therapeutic indication: Daurismo is indicated, in combination with low-dose cytarabine, for the treatment of newly diagnosed de novo or secondary acute myeloid leukaemia (AML) in adult patients who are not candidates for standard induction chemotherapy.
