Name of medicine: Enspryng

International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: satralizumab
Active substance: satralizumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Neuromyelitis Optica
ATC code (human): L04AC
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Satralizumab (Enspryng) is indicated as a monotherapy or in combination with immunosuppressive therapy (IST) for the treatment of neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorders (NMOSD) in adult and adolescent patients from 12 years of age who are anti-aquaporin-4 IgG (AQP4-IgG) seropositive.
