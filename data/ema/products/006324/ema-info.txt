EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006324
Name of medicine: Ryjunea
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: atropine sulfate
Active substance: atropine sulfate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Myopia
ATC code (human): S01FA01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Ophthalmologicals
Therapeutic indication: Ryjunea is indicated for slowing the progression of myopia in paediatric patients. Treatment may be initiated in children aged 3-14 years with a progression rate of 0.5 D or more per year and a severity of -0.5 D to -6.0 D.
