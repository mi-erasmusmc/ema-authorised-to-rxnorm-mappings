EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000229
Name of medicine: Temodal
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: temozolomide
Active substance: temozolomide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Glioma
ATC code (human): L01AX03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Temodal hard capsules is indicated for the treatment of:  adult patients with newly diagnosed glioblastoma multiforme concomitantly with radiotherapy and subsequently as monotherapy treatment; children from the age of three years, adolescents and adult patients with malignant glioma, such as glioblastoma multiforme or anaplastic astrocytoma, showing recurrence or progression after standard therapy.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
