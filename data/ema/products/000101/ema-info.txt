EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000101
Name of medicine: Bondronat
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ibandronic acid
Active substance: ibandronic acid
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypercalcemia;Breast Neoplasms;Neoplasm Metastasis;Fractures, Bone
ATC code (human): M05BA06
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs for treatment of bone diseases
Therapeutic indication: Bondronat is indicated for:  prevention of skeletal events (pathological fractures, bone complications requiring radiotherapy or surgery) in patients with breast cancer and bone metastases; treatment of tumour-induced hypercalcaemia with or without metastases.
