# Ranluspec mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-11.

[FDA approval letter](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2026/761480Orig1s000ltr.pdf) and [FDA Purple Book](https://purplebooksearch.fda.gov/index.cfm?blaNo=761480&event=productdetails) identify Ranluspec as ranibizumab-hkdz, approved 2026-06-02 under BLA 761480. Both 0.5 mg/0.05 mL (10 mg/mL) single-dose vial and prefilled syringe presentations are approved. The EU and US brand names match.

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/ranluspec-epar-product-information_en.pdf) specifies 0.23 mL vial fill and 0.165 mL prefilled syringe fill. The delivered dose is 0.05 mL containing 0.5 mg; excess solution is removed before injection. [FDA label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761480Orig1s000lbl.pdf) likewise describes vial and syringe presentations designed to provide 0.05 mL.

Standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension searches using Ranluspec and ranibizumab-hkdz did not return matching hkdz concepts. Both mappings remain BROAD to unbranded plain ranibizumab, preserving delivered dose, concentration and device: 19080984 (RxCUI 643193), 0.05 ML ranibizumab 10 MG/ML Injection, and 1593222 (RxCUI 1864423), 0.05 ML ranibizumab 10 MG/ML Prefilled Syringe. Other biosimilar suffixes and the originator brand Lucentis are unsuitable.

The vial suggestion was corrected from the placeholder ranibizumab-xxxx to the known ranibizumab-hkdz name. The existing syringe suggestion already uses that suffix. Both suggestions include the matching Ranluspec brand.
