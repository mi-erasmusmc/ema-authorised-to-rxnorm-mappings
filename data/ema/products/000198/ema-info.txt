EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000198
Name of medicine: Zenapax
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: daclizumab
Active substance: daclizumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Graft Rejection;Kidney Transplantation
ATC code (human): L04AA08
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Zenapax is indicated for the prophylaxis of acute organ rejection in de novo allogenic renal transplantation and is to be used concomitantly with an immunosuppressive regimen, including cyclosporine and corticosteroids in patients who are not highly immunised.
Biosimilar: No
