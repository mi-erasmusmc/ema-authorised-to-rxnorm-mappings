EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/001195
Name of medicine: Ibandronic Acid Teva
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ibandronic acid
Active substance: ibandronic acid
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Breast Neoplasms;Neoplasm Metastasis;Fractures, Bone;Osteoporosis, Postmenopausal
ATC code (human): M05BA06
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs for treatment of bone diseases
Therapeutic indication: Ibandronic acid 50mg Ibandronic Acid Teva is indicated for the prevention of skeletal events (pathological fractures, bone complications requiring radiotherapy or surgery) in patients with breast cancer and bone metastases. Ibandronic acid 150mg Treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women at increased risk of fracture. A reduction in the risk of vertebral fractures has been demonstrated, efficacy on femoral neck fractures has not been established.
