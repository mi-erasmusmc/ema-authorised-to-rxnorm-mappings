Therapeutic indication: Treatment of HIV-1 infection Emtricitabine/tenofovir disoproxil Krka is indicated in antiretroviral combination therapy for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adults. Emtricitabine/Tenofovir disoproxil Krka is also indicated for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adolescents, with NRTI resistance or toxicities precluding the use of first line agents, aged 12 to &lt; 18 years (see section 5.1 Pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) Emtricitabine/Tenofovir disoproxil Krka is indicated in combination with safer sex practices for pre-exposure prophylaxis to reduce the risk of sexually acquired HIV-1 infection in adults at high risk.
