EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006007
Name of medicine: Velsipity
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: etrasimod
Active substance: Etrasimod arginine
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Colitis, Ulcerative
ATC code (human): L04AE05
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Velsipity is indicated for the treatment of patients 16 years of age and older with moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis (UC) who have had an inadequate response, lost response, or were intolerant to either conventional therapy, or a biological agent.
