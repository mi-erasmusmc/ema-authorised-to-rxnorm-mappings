# Nylaspeg

[EMA Nylaspeg overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/nylaspeg) identifies a pegfilgrastim biosimilar to Neulasta. [EU register](https://ec.europa.eu/health/documents/community-register/html/h2051.htm) identifies Qilu Pharma Spain as the holder. The local presentation is a 0.6 mL prefilled syringe containing 6 mg (10 mg/mL).

No corresponding FDA-suffixed Qilu/Nylaspeg product was identified in the biosimilar reference or FDA biosimilar list. The newly approved Ennumo is a different product: [FDA approval letter](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/appletter/2026/761147Orig1s000ltr.pdf) identifies Intas manufacturing. Apply the EU-only biosimilar rule: BROAD to plain pegfilgrastim, with a distinct biosimilar suggestion.
