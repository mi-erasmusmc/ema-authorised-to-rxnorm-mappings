EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005113
Name of medicine: Jyseleca
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: filgotinib
Active substance: filgotinib maleate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Arthritis, Rheumatoid
ATC code (human): L04AF04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Rheumatoid arthritis Jyseleca is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe active rheumatoid arthritis in adult patients who have responded inadequately to, or who are intolerant to one or more disease modifying anti rheumatic drugs (DMARDs). Jyseleca may be used as monotherapy or in combination with methotrexate (MTX). Ulcerative colitis Jyseleca is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with moderately to severely active ulcerative colitis who have had an inadequate response with, lost response to, or were intolerant to either conventional therapy or a biologic agent.
