# Invega mapping sources

- The EMA assessment describes the Invega prolonged-release tablets as using an osmotic system designed to deliver paliperidone over 24 hours. This directly supports the `24 HR` release duration in RxNorm, including the 3 mg and 6 mg concepts used for `EU/1/07/395/041` through `EU/1/07/395/048`. [EMA Invega EPAR scientific discussion, section 2](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/scientific-discussion/invega-epar-scientific-discussion_en.pdf#page=1)
- Section 4.2 of the Invega SmPC describes once-daily oral administration and explains the tablet's controlled-release mechanism. [EMA Invega product information, section 4.2](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/invega-epar-product-information_en.pdf#page=2)
