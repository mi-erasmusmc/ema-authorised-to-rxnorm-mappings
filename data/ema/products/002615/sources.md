# Bemfola mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-11 using standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension concept searches.

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/bemfola-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2, confirms that 600 IU/mL is equivalent to 44 micrograms/mL (0.044 mg/mL). The five pen presentations deliver 75 IU in 0.125 mL, 150 IU in 0.25 mL, 225 IU in 0.375 mL, 300 IU in 0.5 mL, and 450 IU in 0.75 mL.

All 15 existing mapping rows were verified against their individual source presentations and retained. Clean unbranded RxNorm Extension pen concepts preserve each volume and the correct concentration, expressed as either 600 UNT/ML or the label-confirmed 0.044 MG/ML equivalent. Standard RxNorm searches did not provide matching pen concepts for these presentations.

No Bemfola-specific FDA-suffixed ingredient was identified in the concept searches. Per the biosimilar mapping rule, all rows remain BROAD to plain follitropin alfa, with suggestions specifying follitropin alfa-xxxx and the Bemfola brand. Originator-branded concepts and pens with 625 UNT/ML are unsuitable. No mapping corrections were needed.
