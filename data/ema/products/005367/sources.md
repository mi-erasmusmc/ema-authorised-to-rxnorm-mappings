Mapping evidence checked 2026-11-06.

- [EMA Skytrofa product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/skytrofa-epar-product-information_en.pdf) defines labeled cartridge strengths in somatropin-equivalent mass (for example 3 mg somatropin equivalent to 8.6 mg lonapegsomatropin). Solvent volume is not a separate delivered dose strength.
- [FDA Purple Book Skytrofa record](https://purplebooksearch.fda.gov/index.cfm?blaNo=761177&event=productdetails) identifies the US ingredient as lonapegsomatropin-tcgd and lists the same single-dose cartridge strengths: 3, 3.6, 4.3, 5.2, 6.3, 7.6, 9.1, 11 and 13.3 mg. These match the standard branded RxNorm cartridge concepts.
