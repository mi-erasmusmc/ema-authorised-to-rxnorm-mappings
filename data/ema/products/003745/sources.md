## Dose equivalence: 340 μg delivered dose vs 400 MCG metered dose

EMA lists aclidinium bromide 340 μg / formoterol 12 μg (delivered dose per actuation). RxNorm lists 0.4 MG/ACTUAT (400 MCG metered dose) / 0.012 MG/ACTUAT (12 MCG). The aclidinium difference (340 vs 400 MCG) reflects delivered dose vs metered dose. Formoterol matches at 12 MCG. Brand name Duaklir confirms correct product match.

### Sources

- FDA prescribing information for Duaklir Pressair confirms: "Each actuation of DUAKLIR PRESSAIR provides a metered dose of 400 mcg of aclidinium bromide" which delivers approximately 340 mcg of aclidinium from the mouthpiece.
