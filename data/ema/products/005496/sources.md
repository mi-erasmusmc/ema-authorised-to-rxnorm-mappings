
Mapping review 2026-11-06.

[EMA Teizeild product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/teizeild-epar-product-information_en.pdf). The label confirms separate single-use 2 mL and 0.5 mL vials at 1 mg/mL. Teplizumab-mzwv is the US name of teplizumab. Existing 2 mL mappings are corrected to the standard unbranded concept because the US brand Tzield differs from Teizeild. The new 0.5 mL vial has no matching volume-specific concept even with Extension and therefore maps BROAD to the concentration-only Injection concept.
