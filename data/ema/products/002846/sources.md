# Sivextro

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/sivextro-epar-product-information_en.pdf) states each sachet contains 245.3 mg tedizolid phosphate and yields 20 mg/mL suspension after constitution. No matching oral suspension concept found in standard RxNorm or Extension. Ingredient BROAD avoids incorrect tablet form or 200 mg strength.
