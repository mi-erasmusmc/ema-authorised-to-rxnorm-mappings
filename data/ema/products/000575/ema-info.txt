EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000575
Name of medicine: Parareg
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: cinacalcet
Active substance: cinacalcet
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypercalcemia;Parathyroid Neoplasms;Hyperparathyroidism
ATC code (human): H05BX01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Calcium homeostasis
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of secondary hyperparathyroidism (HPT) in patients with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) on maintenance dialysis therapy. Mimpara may be used as part of a therapeutic regimen including phosphate binders and/or Vitamin D sterols, as appropriate (see section 5.1). Reduction of hypercalcaemia in patients with:  parathyroid carcinoma. primary HPT for whom parathyroidectomy would be indicated on the basis of serum calciumlevels (as defined by relevant treatment guidelines), but in whom parathyroidectomy is not clinically appropriate or is contraindicated.
Biosimilar: No
