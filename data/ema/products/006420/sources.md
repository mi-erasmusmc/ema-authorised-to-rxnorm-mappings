# Boey

The local authorised-presentations PDF identifies Boey 1400 U powder for solution for injection. [EMA Boey overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/boey) identifies trenibotulinumtoxinE. [AbbVie product monograph](https://www.abbvie.ca/content/dam/abbvie-dotcom/ca/en/documents/products/BOEY_PM_EN.pdf) identifies Clostridium botulinum serotype E neurotoxin, 1400 units per single-use vial.

Standard and Extension searches for brand, trenibotulinumtoxinE, trenibotulinumtoxin E and botulinum toxin type E did not return the ingredient or clinical drug. Results for toxin serotypes A/B or type E antitoxins do not match.
