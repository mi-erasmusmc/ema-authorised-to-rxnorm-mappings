EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005061
Name of medicine: Enerzair Breezhaler
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: indacaterol;glycopyrronium bromide;mometasone
Active substance: indacaterol;glycopyrronium bromide;mometasone
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Asthma
ATC code (human): R03AL
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs for obstructive airway diseases
Therapeutic indication: Enerzair Breezhaler is indicated as a maintenance treatment of asthma in adult patients not adequately controlled with a maintenance combination of a long acting beta2 agonist and a high dose of an inhaled corticosteroid who experienced one or more asthma exacerbations in the previous year.
