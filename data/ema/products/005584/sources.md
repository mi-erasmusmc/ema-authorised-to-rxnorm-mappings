# EURneffy

The local authorised-presentations PDF identifies EURneffy, not the stale withdrawn Neffy metadata. Each device contains one dose: 2 mg or 1 mg in 0.1 mL, corresponding to 20 mg/mL or 10 mg/mL.

[EMA Eurneffy overview](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/eurneffy) confirms that each nasal spray contains a single dose. Standard RxNorm has unbranded concentration-level Nasal Spray concepts at both concentrations. No 0.1 mL volume-specific or 1/2 mg per actuation concept was found in standard or Extension searches. BROAD preserves ingredient, concentration and form while explicitly acknowledging the missing unit-dose volume. The US Neffy brand is not used for the EU EURneffy brand.
