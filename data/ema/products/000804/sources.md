The local authorised-presentations PDF and footnote.txt specify A/turkey/Turkey/1/2005 (H5N1)-like strain NIBRG-23, 7.5 micrograms haemagglutinin per 0.5 mL, rather than the A/VietNam/1194/2004 strain in the older ema-info.txt. The [EMA Aflunov product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/aflunov-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2, confirms this presentation. Convert 7.5 micrograms / 0.5 mL to 0.015 MG/ML and use the unbranded standard RxNorm strain-specific 0.5 ML Prefilled Syringe concept.
