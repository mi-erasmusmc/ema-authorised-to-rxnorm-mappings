# Fulphila

- The pegfilgrastim biosimilar reference table links EU Fulphila to FDA pegfilgrastim-jmdb.
- [DailyMed Fulphila label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=9bfc4d3e-6120-7fec-f1d6-0b3743752034) confirms pegfilgrastim-jmdb 6 mg/0.6 mL in a prefilled syringe. The unbranded FDA-suffixed concept is used under the biosimilar mapping rule.
