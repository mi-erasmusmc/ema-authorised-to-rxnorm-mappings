EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005386
Name of medicine: Phesgo
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: pertuzumab;trastuzumab
Active substance: pertuzumab;trastuzumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Breast Neoplasms
ATC code (human): L01XY
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Early breast cancer (EBC) Phesgo is indicated for use in combination with chemotherapy in:  the neoadjuvant treatment of adult patients with HER2-positive, locally advanced, inflammatory, or early stage breast cancer at high risk of recurrence the adjuvant treatment of adult patients with HER2-positive early breast cancer at high risk of recurrence  Metastatic breast cancer (MBC) Phesgo is indicated for use in combination with docetaxel in adult patients with HER2-positive metastatic or locally recurrent unresectable breast cancer, who have not received previous anti-HER2 therapy or chemotherapy for their metastatic disease.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
