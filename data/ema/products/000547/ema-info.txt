Name of medicine: Zevalin
Medicine status: Lapsed
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ibritumomab tiuxetan
Active substance: ibritumomab tiuxetan
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Lymphoma, Follicular
ATC code (human): V10XX02
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Therapeutic radiopharmaceuticals
Therapeutic indication: Zevalin is indicated in adults. [90Y]-radiolabelled Zevalin is indicated as consolidation therapy after remission induction in previously untreated patients with follicular lymphoma. The benefit of Zevalin following rituximab in combination with chemotherapy has not been established. [90Y]-radiolabelled Zevalin is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with rituximab relapsedorrefractory CD20+ follicular B-cell non-Hodgkin's lymphoma (NHL).
