EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005244
Name of medicine: Koselugo
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: selumetinib
Active substance: selumetinib sulfate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Neurofibromatosis 1
ATC code (human): L01EE04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Koselugo as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of symptomatic, inoperable plexiform neurofibromas (PN) in adult and paediatric patients with neurofibromatosis type 1 (NF1) aged 3 years and older. Koselugo as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of symptomatic, inoperable plexiform neurofibromas (PN) in patients with neurofibromatosis type 1 (NF1) aged 1 year to less than 7 years and for older patients with swallowing difficulties.
