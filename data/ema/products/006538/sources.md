# Fluad mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-11.

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/fluad-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2, confirms the 2026/2027 formulation: A/Missouri/11/2025 (H1N1), A/Darwin/1454/2025 (H3N2)-like represented by A/Michigan/105/2025, and B/Tokyo/EIS13-175/2025. Each contributes 15 micrograms HA per 0.5 mL dose, equivalent to 0.03 MG/ML. The vaccine is MF59C.1-adjuvanted and supplied in prefilled syringes. This agrees with local footnote.txt and parsed_data_2026-07-23.tsv.

Hecate standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension searches for Fluad 2026-2027 and Missouri/Darwin/Tokyo returned older seasonal concepts, including 2025/2026 Croatia/Victoria/Austria, but no matching current formulation. A different season must not be accepted as EXACT. All four presentations use standard concept 46276001 (RxCUI 1657137), the season-independent trivalent antigen combination in a Prefilled Syringe, as BROAD. This also avoids attributing the 2025/2026 brand qualifier to the current product. The generic concept omits the seasonal strains, antigen strengths, single-dose volume and adjuvant; the suggestion captures the current strains, concentrations and volume, with the Fluad brand identifying the adjuvanted product.

The differences between with/without needle and packs of one/ten do not alter the clinical mapping. flag.tsv records the reviewed prior targets and corrections.
