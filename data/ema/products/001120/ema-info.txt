EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/001120
Name of medicine: Prolia
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: denosumab
Active substance: denosumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Bone Resorption;Osteoporosis, Postmenopausal;Osteoporosis
ATC code (human): M05BX04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs for treatment of bone diseases
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women and in men at increased risk of fractures. In postmenopausal women Prolia significantly reduces the risk of vertebral, non vertebral and hip fractures. Treatment of bone loss associated with hormone ablation in men with prostate cancer at increased risk of fractures. In men with prostate cancer receiving hormone ablation, Prolia significantly reduces the risk of vertebral fractures.
