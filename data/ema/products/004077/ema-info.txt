Smouldering&nbsp;multiple&nbsp;myeloma Darzalex&nbsp;as&nbsp;monotherapy&nbsp;is&nbsp;indicated&nbsp;for&nbsp;the treatment of adult patients with smouldering multiple myeloma at high risk of developing multiple myeloma. Light&nbsp;chain&nbsp;(AL)&nbsp;amyloidosis Darzalex is indicated in combination with cyclophosphamide,&nbsp;bortezomib&nbsp;and&nbsp;dexamethasone for the treatment of adult patients with newly diagnosed systemic light chain (AL) amyloidosis.
