# Itvisma

The local authorised-presentations PDF specifies 1.2 × 10^14 vector genomes in 3 mL (4 × 10^13 vg/mL), for intrathecal use. [US Itvisma label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ab2e1c6c-4f95-4243-9296-1734e0a30591) identifies the matching single-dose 3 mL vial and the US ingredient name onasemnogene abeparvovec-brve. Use the matching branded volume-specific standard RxNorm concept; the intravenous Zolgensma product has a different concentration and suffix.
