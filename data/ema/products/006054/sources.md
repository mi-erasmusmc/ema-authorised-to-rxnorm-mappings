
Mapping review 2026-11-06.

[EMA Arexvy product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/arexvy-epar-product-information_en.pdf). For the new presentation, the prefilled syringe holds only adjuvant suspension. Its contents are added to the antigen powder vial; the reconstituted vaccine is then withdrawn into a syringe for administration. Thus the 0.5 ML Injection concept still correctly represents the final vaccine rather than an antigen-containing prefilled syringe.
