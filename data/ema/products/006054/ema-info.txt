EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006054
Name of medicine: Arexvy
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: recombinant respiratory syncytial virus pre-fusion F protein, adjuvanted with AS01E
Active substance: Respiratory Syncytial Virus recombinant glycoprotein F stabilised in the pre-fusion conformation (RSVPreF3) produced in Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO) cells by recombinant DNA technology
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Respiratory Syncytial Virus Infections
ATC code (human): J07
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Vaccines
Therapeutic indication: Arexvy is indicated for active immunisation for the prevention of lower respiratory tract disease (LRTD) caused by respiratory syncytial virus in:  adults 60 years of age and older; adults 50 through 59 years of age who are at increased risk for RSV disease.  The use of this vaccine should be in accordance with official recommendations.&nbsp;
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
