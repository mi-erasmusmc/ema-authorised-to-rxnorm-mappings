EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000796
Name of medicine: Ceplene
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: histamine dihydrochloride
Active substance: histamine dihydrochloride
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Leukemia, Myeloid, Acute
ATC code (human): L03AX14
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunostimulants
Therapeutic indication: Ceplene maintenance therapy is indicated for adult patients with acute myeloid leukaemia in first remission concomitantly treated with interleukin-2 (IL-2). The efficacy of Ceplene has not been fully demonstrated in patients older than age 60.
