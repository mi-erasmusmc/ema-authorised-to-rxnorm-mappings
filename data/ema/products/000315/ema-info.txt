EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000315
Name of medicine: NutropinAq
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: somatropin
Active substance: somatropin
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Turner Syndrome;Dwarfism, Pituitary
ATC code (human): H01AC01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Pituitary and hypothalamic hormones and analogues
Therapeutic indication: Long-term treatment of children with growth failure due to inadequate endogenous growth hormone secretion. Long-term treatment of growth failure associated with Turner syndrome. Treatment of prepubertal children with growth failure associated with chronic renal insufficiency up to the time of renal transplantation. Replacement of endogenous growth hormone in adults with growth hormone deficiency of either childhood or adult-onset etiology. Growth hormone deficiency should be confirmed appropriately prior to treatment.
