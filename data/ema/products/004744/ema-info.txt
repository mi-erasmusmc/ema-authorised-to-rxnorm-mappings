EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004744
Name of medicine: Palynziq
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: pegvaliase
Active substance: pegvaliase
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Phenylketonurias
ATC code (human): A16AB19
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other alimentary tract and metabolism products
Therapeutic indication: Palynziq is indicated for the treatment of patients with phenylketonuria (PKU) aged 16 years and older who have inadequate blood phenylalanine control (blood phenylalanine levels greater than 600 micromol/l) despite prior management with available treatment options.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
