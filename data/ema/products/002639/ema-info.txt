EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002639
Name of medicine: Xtandi
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: enzalutamide
Active substance: enzalutamide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Prostatic Neoplasms
ATC code (human): L02BB04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Endocrine therapy
Therapeutic indication: Xtandi is indicated for:  as monotherapy or in combination with androgen deprivation therapy for the treatment of adult men with high risk biochemical recurrent (BCR) non-metastatic hormone sensitive prostate cancer (nmHSPC) who are unsuitable for salvage radiotherapy (see section 5.1). the treatment of adult men with metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC) in combination with androgen deprivation therapy (see section 5.1). the treatment of adult men with high-risk non-metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (CRPC) (see section 5.1). the treatment of adult men with metastatic CRPC who are asymptomatic or mildly symptomatic after failure of androgen deprivation therapy in whom chemotherapy is not yet clinically indicated (see section 5.1). the treatment of adult men with metastatic CRPC whose disease has progressed on or after docetaxel therapy.
