Name of medicine: Brukinsa
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: zanubrutinib
Active substance: zanubrutinib
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Waldenstrom Macroglobulinemia
ATC code (human): L01EL03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Brukinsa&nbsp;as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with Waldenström’s macroglobulinaemia (WM) who have received at least one prior therapy, or in first line treatment for patients unsuitable for chemo-immunotherapy. Brukinsa&nbsp;as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with marginal zone lymphoma (MZL) who have received at least one prior anti-CD20-based therapy. Brukinsa&nbsp;as monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL). Brukinsa in combination with obinutuzumab is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with refractory or relapsed follicular lymphoma (FL) who have received at least two prior systemic therapies.
