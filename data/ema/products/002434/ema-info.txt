Name of medicine: Fycompa
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: perampanel
Active substance: perampanel
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Epilepsies, Partial
ATC code (human): N03AX22
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antiepileptics;Other antiepileptics
Therapeutic indication: Fycompa is indicated for the adjunctive treatment of partial-onset seizures with or without secondarily generalised seizures in adult and adolescent patients from 12 years of age with epilepsy. Fycompa is indicated for the adjunctive treatment of primary generalised tonic-clonic seizures in adult and adolescent patients from 12 years of age with idiopathic generalised epilepsy.
