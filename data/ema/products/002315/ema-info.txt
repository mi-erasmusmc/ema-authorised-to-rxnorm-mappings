EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002315
Name of medicine: Caprelsa
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: vandetanib
Active substance: vandetanib
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Thyroid Neoplasms
ATC code (human): L01XE
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic and immunomodulating agents
Therapeutic indication: Caprelsa is indicated for the treatment of aggressive and symptomatic medullary thyroid cancer (MTC) in patients with unresectable locally advanced or metastatic disease. Caprelsa is indicated in adults, children and adolescents aged 5 years and older. For patients in whom re-arranged-during-transfection(RET) mutation is not known or is negative, a possible lower benefit should be taken into account before individual treatment decision.
