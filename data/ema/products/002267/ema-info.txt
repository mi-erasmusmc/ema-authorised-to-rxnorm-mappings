Name of medicine: Buccolam
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: midazolam
Active substance: midazolam
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Epilepsy
ATC code (human): N05CD08
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Psycholeptics
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of prolonged, acute, convulsive seizures in infants from 3 months to adults.BUCCOLAM must only be used by parents/carers where the patient has been diagnosed to have epilepsy.For infants between 3-6 months of age treatment should be in a hospital setting where monitoring is possible and resuscitation equipment is available.
