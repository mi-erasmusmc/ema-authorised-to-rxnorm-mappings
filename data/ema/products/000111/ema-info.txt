Name of medicine: LeukoScan
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: sulesomab
Active substance: sulesomab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Osteomyelitis;Radionuclide Imaging
ATC code (human): VO4D
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Diagnostic agents
Therapeutic indication: This medicinal product is for diagnostic use only. LeukoScan is indicated for diagnostic imaging for determining the location and extent of infection/inflammation in bone in patients with suspected osteomyelitis, including patients with diabetic foot ulcers. LeukoScan has not been employed to diagnose osteomyelitis in patients with sickle cell anaemia.
