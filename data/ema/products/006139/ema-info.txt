EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006139
Name of medicine: Eurneffy
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: epinephrine
Active substance: epinephrine
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Anaphylaxis
ATC code (human): C01CA24
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Cardiac therapy
Therapeutic indication: Eurneffy is indicated in the emergency treatment of allergic reactions (anaphylaxis) due to insect stings or bites, foods, medicinal products and other allergens as well as idiopathic or exercise induced anaphylaxis. Treatment is indicated for adults and children aged 4&nbsp;years and over with a body weight of 15&nbsp;kg or more.
