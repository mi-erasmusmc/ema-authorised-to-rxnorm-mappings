# Fluenz

The local presentation PDF and footnote identify live-attenuated trivalent nasal vaccine: 0.2 mL containing nominal 10^7 FFU of each of A/Missouri/11/2025 (H1N1)-like, A/Darwin/1454/2025 (H3N2)-like and B/Tokyo/EIS13-175/2025-like strains. This is nominal 50000000 FFU/mL per strain; FFU is not converted to TCID50 or another infectivity measure.

Standard and Extension searches found no suitable complete current-season clinical concept. The generic trivalent Nasal Spray concept preserves the three influenza subtypes and nasal form, but lacks the current strains, dose and explicit live-attenuated character. Map BROAD; full suggestion follows source strains and potency. [FDA 2026–2027 influenza composition](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/vaccines/influenza-vaccine-composition-2026-2027-us-influenza-season) also lists these target strains for the season.
