EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000561
Name of medicine: Osseor
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: strontium ranelate
Active substance: strontium ranelate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Osteoporosis, Postmenopausal
ATC code (human): M05BX03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs for treatment of bone diseases
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of severe osteoporosis in postmenopausal women at high risk for fracture to reduce the risk of vertebral and hip fractures. Treatment of severe osteoporosis in adult men at increased risk of fracture. The decision to prescribe strontium ranelate should be based on an assessment of the individual patient's overall risks.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
