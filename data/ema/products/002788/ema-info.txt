Name of medicine: Jinarc

Therapeutic area (MeSH): Polycystic Kidney, Autosomal Dominant

Therapeutic indication: Jinarc is indicated to slow the progression of cyst development and renal insufficiency of autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD) in adults with CKD stage 1 to 3 at initiation of treatment with evidence of rapidly progressing disease.
