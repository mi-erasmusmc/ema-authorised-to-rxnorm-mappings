EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005175
Name of medicine: Arsenic trioxide Accord
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: arsenic trioxide
Active substance: arsenic trioxide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Leukemia, Promyelocytic, Acute
ATC code (human): L01XX27
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Arsenic trioxide is indicated for induction of remission, and consolidation in adult patients with:  Newly diagnosed low-to-intermediate risk acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL) (white blood cell count, ? 10 x 103/?l) in combination with all-trans-retinoic acid (ATRA) Relapsed/refractory acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL)(Previous treatment should have included a retinoid and chemotherapy) characterised by the presence of the t(15;17) translocation and/or the presence of the promyelocytic leukaemia/retinoic-acid-receptor-alpha (PML/RAR-alpha) gene.The response rate of other acute myelogenous leukaemia subtypes to arsenic trioxide has not been examined.
