Therapeutic indication: Clopidogrel/Acetylsalicylic acid Teva is indicated for the prevention of atherothrombotic events in adult patients already taking both clopidogrel and acetylsalicylic acid (ASA). Clopidogrel/Acetylsalicylic acid Teva is a fixed?dose combination medicinal product for continuation of therapy in:  Non?ST segment elevation acute coronary syndrome (unstable angina or non?Q?wave myocardial infarction) including patients undergoing a stent placement following percutaneous coronary intervention  ST segment elevation acute myocardial infarction in medically treated patients eligible for thrombolytic therapy
