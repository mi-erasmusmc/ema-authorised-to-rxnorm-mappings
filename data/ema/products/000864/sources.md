The local authorised-presentations PDF lists 250 mg powder in a 30 mL vial and 750 mg powder in a 50 mL vial. The [EMA Vibativ product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/vibativ-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 6.6, describes the powder strengths and reconstitution. The listed vial capacities are not the reconstituted solution volumes; use standard RxNorm total-strength Injection concepts.
