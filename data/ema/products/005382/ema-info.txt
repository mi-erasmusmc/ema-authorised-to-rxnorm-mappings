EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005382
Name of medicine: Lydisilka
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: estetrol;drospirenone
Active substance: estetrol monohydrate;drospirenone
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Contraceptives, Oral
ATC code (human): G03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Sex hormones and modulators of the genital system
Therapeutic indication: Oral contraception. The decision to prescribe Lydisilka should take into consideration the individual woman’s current risk factors, particularly those for venous thromboembolism (VTE), and how the risk of VTE with Lydisilka compares with other combined hormonal contraceptives (CHCs) (see sections 4.3 and 4.4).
