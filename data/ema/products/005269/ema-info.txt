EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005269
Name of medicine: Kaftrio
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ivacaftor;tezacaftor;elexacaftor
Active substance: ivacaftor;tezacaftor;elexacaftor
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Cystic Fibrosis
ATC code (human): R07AX32
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other respiratory system products
Therapeutic indication: Kaftrio tablets are indicated in a combination regimen with ivacaftor for the treatment of cystic fibrosis (CF) in patients aged 6 years and older who have at least one non-Class I mutation in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) gene. Kaftrio granules are indicated in a combination regimen with ivacaftor for the treatment of cystic fibrosis (CF) in paediatric patients aged 2 to less than 6 years who have at least one non-Class I mutation in the cystic fibrosis transmembrane conductance regulator (CFTR) gene.
