# Zefylti

- [EMA authorised Zefylti product page](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/zefylti) explicitly identifies the product as a biosimilar, EMEA/H/C/006400. The local metadata incorrectly says Biosimilar: No and refers to an older withdrawn application; the presentation PDF identifies the authorised EU/1/24/1899 series.
- [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/zefylti-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2: 30 MU equals 300 micrograms in 0.5 mL (0.6 mg/mL), and 48 MU equals 480 micrograms in 0.5 mL (0.96 mg/mL).
- No Zefylti FDA-suffixed equivalent was identified in the filgrastim biosimilar reference or [FDA biosimilar list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/biosimilars/biosimilar-product-information). Apply the EU-only biosimilar rule: BROAD to plain filgrastim with a product-specific biosimilar suggestion.
- Standard RxNorm captures the 0.5 mL, 0.6 mg/mL syringe; Extension is needed for the matching 0.5 mL, 0.96 mg/mL syringe. Reject standard 0.48 mg/mL concepts because those represent a different concentration.
