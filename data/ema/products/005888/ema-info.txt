Name of medicine: Zefylti
Medicine status: Application withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: filgrastim
Active substance: filgrastim
Therapeutic area (MeSH): 
ATC code (human): L03AA02
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): filgrastim
Therapeutic indication: Zefylti is indicated for the reduction in the duration of neutropenia and the incidence of febrile neutropenia in patients treated with established cytotoxic chemotherapy for malignancy (with the exception of chronic myeloid leukaemia and myelodysplastic syndromes) and for the reduction in the duration of neutropenia in patients undergoing myeloablative therapy followed by bone marrow transplantation considered to be at increased risk of prolonged severe neutropenia.
