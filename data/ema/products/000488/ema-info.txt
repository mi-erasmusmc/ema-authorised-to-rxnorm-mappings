Name of medicine: Vivanza
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: vardenafil
Active substance: vardenafil
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Erectile Dysfunction
ATC code (human): G04BE09
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Urologicals
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of erectile dysfunction in adult men. Erectile dysfunction is the inability to achieve or maintain a penile erection sufficient for satisfactory sexual performance. In order for Vivanza to be effective, sexual stimulation is required. Vivanza is not indicated for use by women.
