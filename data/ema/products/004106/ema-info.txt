International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: venetoclax

Therapeutic area (MeSH): Leukemia, Lymphocytic, Chronic, B-Cell
ATC code (human): L01XX52
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Venclyxto is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with previously untreated chronic lymphocytic leukaemia (CLL):  in combination with acalabrutinib with or without obinutuzumab&nbsp; in combination with obinutuzumab in combination with ibrutinib&nbsp;&nbsp;  Venclyxto in combination with rituximab is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with CLL who have received at least one prior therapy.&nbsp;&nbsp; Venclyxto monotherapy is indicated for the treatment of CLL:  in the presence of 17p deletion or TP53 mutation in adult patients who are unsuitable for or have failed a B cell receptor pathway inhibitor, or in the absence of 17p deletion or TP53 mutation in adult patients who have failed both chemoimmunotherapy and a B cell receptor pathway inhibitor.&nbsp;&nbsp;  Venclyxto in combination with a hypomethylating agent is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with newly diagnosed acute myeloid leukaemia (AML) who are ineligible for intensive chemotherapy.
