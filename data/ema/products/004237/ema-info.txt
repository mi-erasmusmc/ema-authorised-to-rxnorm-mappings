EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004237
Name of medicine: Xerava
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: eravacycline
Active substance: eravacycline
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Infection;Bacterial Infections
ATC code (human): J01AA13
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Tetracyclines
Therapeutic indication: Xerava is indicated in adolescents from the age of 12 years weighing at least 50 kg, and in adults, for the treatment of complicated intra-abdominal infections (cIAI). Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
