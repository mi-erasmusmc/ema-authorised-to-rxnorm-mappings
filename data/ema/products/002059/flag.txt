Komboglyze is an EU-only immediate-release saxagliptin/metformin formulation. The US equivalent (Kombiglyze XR) is Extended Release only. No standard RxNorm IR concepts exist.

Mapped using RxNorm Extension concepts:
- 850 mg: Metformin 850 MG / saxagliptin 2.5 MG Oral Tablet (OMOP351572)
- 1000 mg: Metformin 1000 MG / saxagliptin 2.5 MG Oral Tablet (OMOP417869)
