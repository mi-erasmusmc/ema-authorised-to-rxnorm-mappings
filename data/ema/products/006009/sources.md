# Agilus mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-11. Both existing BROAD mappings were checked and retained.

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/agilus-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 6.6, specifies 120 mg dantrolene sodium hemiheptahydrate per vial. Adding 20 mL water gives a final reconstituted volume of 22.6 mL and a labelled concentration of 5.3 mg/mL. The product is for single use only, with residual solution discarded.

[EMA assessment report](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/agilus-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf), pharmaceutical development, confirms these reconstitution details and distinguishes Agilus from the 20 mg Dantrium IV reference presentation. The two EMA presentations differ only in pack size (6 versus 10 vials).

Standard RxNorm searches found dantrolene Injection, concept 42629601 (RxCUI 1791243), alongside strength-specific concepts for other presentations (250 mg injection and 0.333 mg/mL injectable solution). Neither strength matches Agilus.

Extension search, expanded to 100 candidates, found dantrolene 120 MG Injectable Solution (854221; OMOP5577130) and dantrolene 120 MG (854222; OMOP5577131). These names do not establish whether their strength denotes the labelled sodium hemiheptahydrate or dantrolene base. The solution concept also uses the multi-use Injectable Solution form, while Agilus is single use. The strength basis has not been verified, so these candidates are not accepted as stronger matches.

The existing standard dantrolene Injection concept avoids asserting an unverified salt-to-base strength equivalence and preserves the single-use injection form. Both rows remain BROAD; suggestions retain the labelled 120 mg sodium hemiheptahydrate Agilus presentation.
