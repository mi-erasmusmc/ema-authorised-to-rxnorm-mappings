EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000675
Name of medicine: Avaglim
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: rosiglitazone;glimepiride
Active substance: rosiglitazone;glimepiride
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Diabetes Mellitus, Type 2
ATC code (human): A10BD04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs used in diabetes
Therapeutic indication: AVAGLIM is indicated in the treatment of type 2 diabetes mellitus patients who are unable to achieve sufficient glycaemic control on optimal dosage of sulphonylurea monotherapy, and for whom metformin is inappropriate because of contraindication or intolerance.
