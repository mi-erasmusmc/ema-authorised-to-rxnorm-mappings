EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002697
Name of medicine: Opsumit
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: macitentan
Active substance: macitentan
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypertension, Pulmonary
ATC code (human): C02KX04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antihypertensives
Therapeutic indication: Adults&nbsp;Opsumit, as monotherapy or in combination, is indicated for the long-term treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in adult patients of WHO Functional Class (FC) II to III. Paediatric populationOpsumit, as monotherapy or in combination, is indicated for the long-term treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in paediatric patients aged less than 18 years and bodyweight ≥ 40 kg with WHO Functional Class (FC) II to III. Opsumit, as monotherapy or in combination, is indicated for the long-term treatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) in paediatric patients aged 2 years to less than 18 years with WHO Functional Class (FC) II to III.
