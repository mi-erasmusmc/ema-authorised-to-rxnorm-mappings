--²³: COMIRNATY LP.8.1 30 micrograms/dose dispersion for injection (EU/1/20/1528/045-047, EU/1/20/1528/051):
1 dose (0.3 mL) contains 30 micrograms mRNA encoding LP.8.1, COVID-19 mRNA Vaccine (nucleoside modified, embedded in lipid
nanoparticles).
The mRNA encoding LP.8.1 is a single-stranded, 5’-capped messenger RNA (mRNA) produced using a cell-free in vitro transcription from
the corresponding DNA templates, encoding the viral spike (S) protein of SARS-CoV-2 (Omicron LP.8.1).
