nanoparticles).
Bretovameran is a single-stranded, 5’-capped messenger RNA (mRNA) produced using a cell-free in vitro transcription from the
corresponding DNA templates, encoding the viral spike (S) protein of SARS-CoV-2 (Omicron JN.1).
