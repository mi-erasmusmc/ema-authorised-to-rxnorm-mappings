EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005107
Name of medicine: Roclanda
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: latanoprost / netarsudil
Active substance: Latanoprost;netarsudil mesilate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Glaucoma, Open-Angle;Ocular Hypertension
ATC code (human): S01EE
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Ophthalmologicals
Therapeutic indication: Roclanda is indicated for the reduction of elevated intraocular pressure (IOP) in adult patients with primary open-angle glaucoma or ocular hypertension for whom monotherapy with a prostaglandin or netarsudil provides insufficient IOP reduction.
