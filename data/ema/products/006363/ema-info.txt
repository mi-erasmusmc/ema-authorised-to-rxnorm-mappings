EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006363
Name of medicine: Romvimza
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: vimseltinib
Active substance: vimseltinib dihydrate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Giant Cell Tumor of Tendon Sheath
ATC code (human): L01EX
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Romvimza is indicated for treatment of adult patients with symptomatic tenosynovial giant cell tumour (TGCT) associated with clinically relevant physical function deterioration and in whom surgical options have been exhausted or would induce unacceptable morbidity or disability.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
