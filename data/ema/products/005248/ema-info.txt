EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005248
Name of medicine: Tecovirimat SIGA
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: tecovirimat monohydrate
Active substance: tecovirimat
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Poxviridae Infections;Cowpox;Monkeypox;Vaccinia;Smallpox
ATC code (human): J05AX24
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antivirals for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Tecovirimat SIGA is indicated for the treatment of the following viral infections in adults and children with body weight at least 13 kg:  Smallpox Monkeypox Cowpox  Tecovirimat SIGA is also indicated to treat complications due to replication of vaccinia virus following vaccination against smallpox in adults and children with body weight at least 13 kg (see sections 4.4 and 5.1). Tecovirimat SIGA should be used in accordance with official recommendations.
