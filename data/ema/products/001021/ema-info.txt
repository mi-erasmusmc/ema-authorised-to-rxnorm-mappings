Name of medicine: Adcirca (previously Tadalafil Lilly)
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: tadalafil
Active substance: tadalafil
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypertension, Pulmonary
ATC code (human): G04BE08
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Urologicals
Therapeutic indication: AdultsTreatment of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) classified as WHO functional class II and III, to improve exercise capacity (see section 5.1). Efficacy has been shown in idiopathic PAH (IPAH) and in PAH related to collagen vascular disease. Paediatric populationTreatment of paediatric patients aged 2 years and above with pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) classified as WHO functional class II and III.
