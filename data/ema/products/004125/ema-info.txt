EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004125
Name of medicine: Onivyde pegylated liposomal (previously Onivyde)
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: irinotecan hydrochloride trihydrate
Active substance: irinotecan anhydrous free-base
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Pancreatic Neoplasms
ATC code (human): L01CE02
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: ONIVYDE pegylated liposomal is indicated:- in combination with oxaliplatin, 5 fluorouracil (5 FU) and leucovorin (LV) for the first-line treatment of adult patients with metastatic adenocarcinoma of the pancreas,- in combination with 5-FU and LV for the treatment of metastatic adenocarcinoma of the pancreas, in adult patients who have progressed following gemcitabine based therapy.
