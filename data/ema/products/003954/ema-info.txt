EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003954
Name of medicine: Orkambi
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: lumacaftor;ivacaftor
Active substance: Lumacaftor;ivacaftor
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Cystic Fibrosis
ATC code (human): R07AX30
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other respiratory system products
Therapeutic indication: Orkambi tablets are indicated for the treatment of cystic fibrosis (CF) in patients aged 6 years and older who are homozygous for the F508del mutation in the CFTR gene. Orkambi granules are indicated for the treatment of cystic fibrosis (CF) in children aged 1 year and older who are homozygous for the F508del mutation in the CFTR gene.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
