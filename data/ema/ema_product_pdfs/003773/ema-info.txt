EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003773
Name of medicine: Kengrexal
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: cangrelor
Active substance: cangrelor
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Acute Coronary Syndrome;Vascular Surgical Procedures
ATC code (human): B01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antithrombotic agents
Therapeutic indication: Kengrexal, co-administered with acetylsalicylic acid (ASA), is indicated for the reduction of thrombotic cardiovascular events in adult patients with coronary artery disease undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) who have not received an oral P2Y12 inhibitor prior to the PCI procedure and in whom oral therapy with P2Y12 inhibitors is not feasible or desirable.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
