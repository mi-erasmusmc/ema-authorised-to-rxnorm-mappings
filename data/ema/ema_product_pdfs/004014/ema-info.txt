EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004014
Name of medicine: Cinacalcet Viatris (previously Cinacalcet Mylan)
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: cinacalcet
Active substance: cinacalcet hydrochloride
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hyperparathyroidism, Secondary;Hypercalcemia
ATC code (human): H05BX01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Calcium homeostasis
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of secondary hyperparathyroidism (HPT) in patients with end-stage renal disease (ESRD) on maintenance dialysis therapy. Cinacalcet Mylan may be used as part of a therapeutic regimen including phosphate binders and/or vitamin D sterols, as appropriate. Reduction of hypercalcaemia in patients with:  parathyroid carcinoma primary HPT for whom parathyroidectomy  would be indicated on the basis of serum calcium levels (as defined by relevant treatment guidelines), but in whom parathyroidectomy is not clinically appropriate or is contraindicated.  
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: Yes
