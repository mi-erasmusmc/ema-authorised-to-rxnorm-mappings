EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002629
Name of medicine: Lixiana
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: edoxaban
Active substance: edoxaban tosilate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Stroke;Venous Thromboembolism
ATC code (human): B01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antithrombotic agents
Therapeutic indication: Prevention of stroke and systemic embolism in adult patients with nonvalvular atrial fibrillation (NVAF) with one or more risk factors, such as congestive heart failure, hypertension, age ? 75 years, diabetes mellitus, prior stroke or transient ischaemic attack (TIA). Treatment of deep vein thrombosis (DVT) and pulmonary embolism (PE), and prevention of recurrent DVT and PE in adults.
Biosimilar: No
