Therapeutic indication: Hemlibra is indicated for routine prophylaxis of bleeding episodes in patients with haemophilia A (congenital factor VIII deficiency):  with factor VIII inhibitors without factor VIII inhibitors who have:  severe disease (FVIII &lt; 1%) moderate disease (FVIII ? 1% and ? 5%) with severe bleeding phenotype.    Hemlibra can be used in all age groups.
