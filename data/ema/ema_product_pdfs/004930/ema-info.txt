EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004930
Name of medicine: Giapreza
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: angiotensin II
Active substance: angiotensin II acetate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypotension;Shock
ATC code (human): C09
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Agents acting on the renin-angiotensin system
Therapeutic indication: Giapreza is indicated for the treatment of refractory hypotension in adults with septic or other distributive shock who remain hypotensive despite adequate volume restitution and application of catecholamines and other available vasopressor therapies.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
