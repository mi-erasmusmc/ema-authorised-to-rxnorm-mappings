EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004134
Name of medicine: Caspofungin Accord
Medicine status: Expired
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: caspofungin
Active substance: caspofungin acetate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Candidiasis;Aspergillosis
ATC code (human): J02AX04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antimycotics for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of invasive candidiasis in adult or paediatric patients. Treatment of invasive aspergillosis in adult or paediatric patients who are refractory to or intolerant of amphotericin B, lipid formulations of amphotericin B and/or itraconazole. Refractoriness is defined as progression of infection or failure to improve after a minimum of 7 days of prior therapeutic doses of effective antifungal therapy. Empirical therapy for presumed fungal infections (such as Candida or Aspergillus) in febrile, neutropaenic adult or paediatric patients.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: Yes
