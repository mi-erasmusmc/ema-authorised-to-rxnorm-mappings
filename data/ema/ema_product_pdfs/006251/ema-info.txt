EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006251
Name of medicine: Dasatinib Accord Healthcare
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: dasatinib
Active substance: Dasatinib monohydrate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Precursor Cell Lymphoblastic Leukemia-Lymphoma;Leukemia, Myelogenous, Chronic, BCR-ABL Positive
ATC code (human): L01EA02
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Dasatinib Accord Healthcare is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with:  newly diagnosed Philadelphia chromosome positive (Ph+) chronic myelogenous leukaemia (CML) in the chronic phase. chronic, accelerated or blast phase CML with resistance or intolerance to prior therapy including imatinib. Ph+ acute lymphoblastic leukaemia (ALL) and lymphoid blast CML with resistance or intolerance to prior therapy.  &nbsp;Dasatinib Accord Healthcare is indicated for the treatment of paediatric patients with:  newly diagnosed Ph+ CML in chronic phase (Ph+ CML-CP) or Ph+ CML-CP resistant or intolerant to prior therapy including imatinib. newly diagnosed Ph+ ALL in combination with chemotherapy.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: Yes
