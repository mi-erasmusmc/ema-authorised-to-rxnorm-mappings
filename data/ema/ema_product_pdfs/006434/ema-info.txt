EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/006434
Name of medicine: Bildyos
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: denosumab
Active substance: denosumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Bone Resorption;Osteoporosis, Postmenopausal;Osteoporosis
ATC code (human): M05BX04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs for treatment of bone diseases
Therapeutic indication: Treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women and in men at increased risk of fractures. Inpostmenopausal women, denosumab significantly reduces the risk of vertebral, non-vertebral and hipfractures. Treatment of bone loss associated with hormone ablation in men with prostate cancer at increased riskof fractures. In men with prostate cancer receiving hormone ablation, denosumabsignificantly reduces the risk of vertebral fractures. Treatment of bone loss associated with long-term systemic glucocorticoid therapy in adult patients atincreased risk of fracture.
Biosimilar: Yes
Generic or hybrid: No
