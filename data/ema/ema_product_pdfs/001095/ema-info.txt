EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/001095
Name of medicine: Menveo
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: meningococcal group A, C, W-135 and Y conjugate vaccine
Active substance: meningococcal group A, C, W-135 and Y conjugate vaccine
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Immunization;Meningitis, Meningococcal
ATC code (human): J07AH08
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Bacterial vaccines
Therapeutic indication: Vials Menveo is indicated for active immunisation of children (from two years of age), adolescents and adults at risk of exposure to Neisseria meningitidis groups A, C, W135 and Y, to prevent invasive disease. The use of this vaccine should be in accordance with official recommendations.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
