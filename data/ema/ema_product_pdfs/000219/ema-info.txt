EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000219
Name of medicine: Ammonaps
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: sodium phenylbutyrate
Active substance: sodium phenylbutyrate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Ornithine Carbamoyltransferase Deficiency Disease;Citrullinemia;Carbamoyl-Phosphate Synthase I Deficiency Disease
ATC code (human): A16AX03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other alimentary tract and metabolism products
Therapeutic indication: Ammonaps is indicated as adjunctive therapy in the chronic management of urea cycle disorders, involving deficiencies of carbamylphosphate synthetase, ornithine transcarbamylase orargininosuccinate synthetase. It is indicated in all patients with neonatal-onset presentation (complete enzyme deficiencies, presenting within the first 28 days of life). It is also indicated in patients with late-onset disease(partial enzyme deficiencies, presenting after the first month of life) who have a history of hyperammonaemic encephalopathy.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
