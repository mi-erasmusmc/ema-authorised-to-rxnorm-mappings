EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004874
Name of medicine: Adakveo
Medicine status: Revoked
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: crizanlizumab
Active substance: Crizanlizumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Anemia, Sickle Cell
ATC code (human): B06AX01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other hematological agents
Therapeutic indication: Adakveo is indicated for the prevention of recurrent vaso occlusive crises (VOCs) in sickle cell disease patients aged 16 years and older. It can be given as an add on therapy to hydroxyurea/hydroxycarbamide (HU/HC) or as monotherapy in patients for whom HU/HC is inappropriate or inadequate.
Biosimilar: No
