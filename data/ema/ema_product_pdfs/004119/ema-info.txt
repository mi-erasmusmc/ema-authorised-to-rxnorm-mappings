EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004119
Name of medicine: Besponsa
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: inotuzumab ozogamicin
Active substance: Inotuzumab ozogamicin
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Precursor Cell Lymphoblastic Leukemia-Lymphoma
ATC code (human): L01FX06
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Besponsa is indicated as monotherapy for the treatment of adults with relapsed or refractory CD22-positive B cell precursor acute lymphoblastic leukaemia (ALL). Adult patients with Philadelphia chromosome positive (Ph+) relapsed or refractory B cell precursor ALL should have failed treatment with at least 1 tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI).
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
