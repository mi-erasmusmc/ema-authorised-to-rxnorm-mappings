EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004692
Name of medicine: Lyvdelzi (previously Seladelpar Gilead)
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: seladelpar lysine dihydrate
Active substance: seladelpar lysine dihydrate
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Liver Cirrhosis, Biliary
ATC code (human): A05AX07
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Bile and liver therapy
Therapeutic indication: Lyvdelzi is indicated for the treatment of primary biliary cholangitis (PBC) in combination with ursodeoxycholic acid (UDCA) in adults who have an inadequate response to UDCA alone, or as monotherapy in those unable to tolerate UDCA.
