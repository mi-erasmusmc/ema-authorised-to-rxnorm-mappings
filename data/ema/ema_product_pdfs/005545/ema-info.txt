EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005545
Name of medicine: Byooviz
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ranibizumab
Active substance: ranibizumab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Wet Macular Degeneration;Macular Edema;Diabetic Retinopathy;Myopia, Degenerative
ATC code (human): S01LA04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Ophthalmologicals
Therapeutic indication: Byooviz is indicated in adults for:  The treatment of neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (AMD) The treatment of visual impairment due to diabetic macular oedema (DME) The treatment of proliferative diabetic retinopathy (PDR) The treatment of visual impairment due to macular oedema secondary to retinal vein occlusion (branch RVO or central RVO)  The treatment of visual impairment due to choroidal neovascularisation (CNV)
Biosimilar: Yes
Generic or hybrid: No
