EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005935
Name of medicine: Sugammadex Amomed
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: sugammadex
Active substance: sugammadex sodium
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Neuromuscular Blockade
ATC code (human): V03AB35
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): All other therapeutic products
Therapeutic indication: Reversal of neuromuscular blockade induced by rocuronium or vecuronium. For the paediatric population: sugammadex is only recommended for routine reversal of rocuronium induced blockade in paediatric patients from birth to 17 years.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: Yes
