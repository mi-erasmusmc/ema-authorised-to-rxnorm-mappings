Name of medicine: Imprida
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: amlodipine;valsartan
Active substance: valsartan;amlodipine (as amlodipine besilate)
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Hypertension
ATC code (human): C09DB01
