Name of medicine: Refludan

Active substance: lepirudin
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Thromboembolism;Thrombocytopenia

Therapeutic indication: Anticoagulation in adult patients with heparin-induced thrombocytopenia type II and thromboembolic disease mandating parenteral antithrombotic therapy. The diagnosis should be confirmed by the heparin-induced platelet activation assay or an equivalent test.
