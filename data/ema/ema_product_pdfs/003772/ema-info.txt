EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003772
Name of medicine: Zerbaxa
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ceftolozane;tazobactam
Active substance: ceftolozane sulfate;tazobactam sodium
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Bacterial Infections
ATC code (human): J01DI54
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antibacterials for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: Zerbaxa is indicated for the treatment of the following infections in adults:  Complicated intra abdominal infections; Acute pyelonephritis; Complicated urinary tract infections; Hospital-acquired pneumonia (HAP), including ventilator associated pneumonia (VAP).  Consideration should be given to official guidance on the appropriate use of antibacterial agents.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
