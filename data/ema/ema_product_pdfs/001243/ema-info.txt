EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/001243
Name of medicine: Pravafenix
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: fenofibrate;pravastatin
Active substance: fenofibrate;pravastatin
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Dyslipidemias
ATC code (human): C10BA03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Lipid modifying agents
Therapeutic indication: Pravafenix is indicated as an adjunct to diet and other non-pharmacological treatment (e.g. exercise, weight reduction) for the treatment of mixed hyperlipidaemia in adult patients at high cardiovascular risk to reduce triglycerides and increase HDL C when LDL C levels are adequately controlled while on a treatment with pravastatin 40 mg monotherapy or on another moderate-intensity statin regimen.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
