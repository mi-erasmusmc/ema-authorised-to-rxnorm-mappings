EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/002720
Name of medicine: Translarna
Medicine status: Expired
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ataluren
Active substance: ataluren
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Muscular Dystrophy, Duchenne
ATC code (human): M09AX03
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Other drugs for disorders of the musculo-skeletal system
Therapeutic indication: Translarna is indicated for the treatment of Duchenne muscular dystrophy resulting from a nonsense mutation in the dystrophin gene, in ambulatory patients aged 2 years and older. Efficacy has not been demonstrated in non-ambulatory patients. The presence of a nonsense mutation in the dystrophin gene should be determined by genetic testing.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
