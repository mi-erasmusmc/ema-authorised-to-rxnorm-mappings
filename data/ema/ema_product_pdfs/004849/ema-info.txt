EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004849
Name of medicine: Idefirix
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: imlifidase
Active substance: imlifidase
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Desensitization, Immunologic;Kidney Transplantation
ATC code (human): L04AA
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Immunosuppressants
Therapeutic indication: Idefirix is indicated for desensitisation treatment of highly sensitised adult kidney transplant patients with positive crossmatch against an available deceased donor. The use of Idefirix should be reserved for patients unlikely to be transplanted under the available kidney allocation system including prioritisation programmes for highly sensitised patients.
