EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004315
Name of medicine: Steglatro
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: ertugliflozin
Active substance: ertugliflozin l-pyroglutamic acid
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Diabetes Mellitus, Type 2
ATC code (human): A10BK04
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs used in diabetes;Sodium-glucose co-transporter 2 (SGLT2) inhibitors
Therapeutic indication: Steglatro is indicated in adults aged 18 years and older with type 2 diabetes mellitus as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycaemic control:  as monotherapy in patients for whom the use of metformin is considered inappropriate due to intolerance or contraindications. in addition to other medicinal products for the treatment of diabetes.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
