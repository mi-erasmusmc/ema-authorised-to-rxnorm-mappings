EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/003904
Name of medicine: Evotaz
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: atazanavir;cobicistat
Active substance: cobicistat;atazanavir
Therapeutic area (MeSH): HIV Infections
ATC code (human): J05AR15
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antivirals for systemic use
Therapeutic indication: EVOTAZ is indicated in combination with other antiretroviral medicinal products for the treatment of HIV-1 infected adults and adolescents (aged 12 years and older weighing at least 35 kg) without known mutations associated with resistance to atazanavir (see sections 4.4 and 5.1).
