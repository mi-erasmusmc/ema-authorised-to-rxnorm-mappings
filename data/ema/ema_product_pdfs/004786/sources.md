## Inbrija (levodopa) - Mapping Sources

### Delivered dose vs capsule fill verification

EMA labels Inbrija capsules as 33 mg (delivered dose), while RxNorm uses 42 mg (capsule fill weight).
Each capsule contains 42 mg levodopa, with ~33 mg delivered via inhalation and ~9 mg remaining in the capsule.

These are the same product — the strength difference is a labelling convention, not a formulation difference.
