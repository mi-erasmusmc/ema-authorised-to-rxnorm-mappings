EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/000201
Name of medicine: Insuman
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: insulin human
Active substance: Insulin human
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Diabetes Mellitus
ATC code (human): A10AB01;A10AC01
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs used in diabetes
Therapeutic indication: Diabetes mellitus where treatment with insulin is required. Insuman Rapid is also suitable for the treatment of hyperglycaemic coma and ketoacidosis, as well as for achieving pre-, intra- and postoperative stabilisation in patients with diabetes mellitus.
