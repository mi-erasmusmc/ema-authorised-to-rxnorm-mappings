EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/004910
Name of medicine: Qtrilmet
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: metformin hydrochloride;saxagliptin;dapagliflozin
Active substance: metformin hydrochloride;saxagliptin;dapagliflozin
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Diabetes Mellitus, Type 2
ATC code (human): A10BD
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Drugs used in diabetes
Therapeutic indication: Qtrilmet is indicated in adults aged 18 years and older with type 2 diabetes mellitus:  to improve glycaemic control when metformin with or without sulphonylurea (SU) and either saxagliptin or dapagliflozin does not provide adequate glycaemic control. when already being treated with metformin and saxagliptin and dapagliflozin.
Biosimilar: No
Generic or hybrid: No
