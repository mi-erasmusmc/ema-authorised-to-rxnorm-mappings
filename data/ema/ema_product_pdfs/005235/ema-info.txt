Name of medicine: Arsenic trioxide Mylan
Medicine status: Withdrawn
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: arsenic trioxide
Active substance: arsenic trioxide
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Leukemia, Promyelocytic, Acute
ATC code (human): L01XX27
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Antineoplastic agents
Therapeutic indication: Arsenic trioxide Mylan is indicated for induction of remission, and consolidation in adult patients with:- Newly diagnosed low to intermediate risk acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL) (white blood cell count, ? 10 x 103/?l) in combination with all trans retinoic acid (ATRA)- Relapsed/refractory acute promyelocytic leukaemia (APL) (Previous treatment should have included a retinoid and chemotherapy)characterised by the presence of the t(15;17) translocation and/or the presence of the promyelocytic leukaemia/retinoic acid receptor alpha (PML/RAR alpha) gene. The response rate of other acute myelogenous leukaemia subtypes to arsenic trioxide has not beenexamined.
