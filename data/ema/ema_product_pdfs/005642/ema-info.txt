EMA product number: EMEA/H/C/005642
Name of medicine: Vabysmo
Medicine status: Authorised
International non-proprietary name (INN) / common name: faricimab
Active substance: Faricimab
Therapeutic area (MeSH): Wet Macular Degeneration;Macular Edema;Diabetes Complications
ATC code (human): S01L
Pharmacotherapeutic group (human): Ophthalmologicals
Therapeutic indication: Vabysmo is indicated for the treatment of adult patients with:  neovascular (wet) age-related macular degeneration (nAMD), visual impairment due to diabetic macular oedema (DME), visual impairment due to macular oedema secondary to retinal vein occlusion (branch RVO or central RVO).
Biosimilar: No
