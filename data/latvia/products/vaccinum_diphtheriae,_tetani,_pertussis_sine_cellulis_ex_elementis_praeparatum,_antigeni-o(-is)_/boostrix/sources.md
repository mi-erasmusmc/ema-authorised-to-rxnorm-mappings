# Boostrix mapping review

Reviewed 2026-11-11.

The [Latvian ZVA SmPC](https://dati.zva.gov.lv/zalu-registrs/info/07-0236/smpc), sections 2 and 3, confirms PT/FHA/pertactin 8/8/2.5 micrograms, diphtheria/tetanus 2.5/5 Lf per 0.5 ml prefilled syringe. The product is a reduced-antigen diphtheria/tetanus/acellular pertussis booster without polio, hepatitis B or Hib.

Selected standard RxNorm concept 42800267 (RxCUI 1300370), EXACT:

`0.5 ML Bordetella pertussis filamentous hemagglutinin vaccine, inactivated 0.016 MG/ML / Bordetella pertussis pertactin vaccine, inactivated 0.005 MG/ML / Bordetella pertussis toxoid vaccine, inactivated 0.016 MG/ML / diphtheria toxoid vaccine, inactivated 5 UNT/ML / tetanus toxoid vaccine, inactivated 10 UNT/ML Prefilled Syringe [Boostrix]`

Each concentration is twice the per-dose content, because the dose is 0.5 ml; micrograms are converted to mg. RxNorm toxoid UNT values reflect the labeled Lf content. EU minimum potency in IU is a separate measurement and must not be treated as numerically interchangeable with Lf. Needle accessories and pack counts do not change the clinical concept.
