# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-20.

[CIMA Fylrevy SmPC, sections 2 and 3](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1262020001/FT_1262020001.html) confirms film-coated oral tablets containing 14.2 mg or 18.9 mg estetrol, as estetrol monohydrate. The labeled strengths refer to estetrol, so no hydrate conversion is needed for mapping.

Hecate searches of standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension for both tablet strengths, Fylrevy, and estetrol returned no suitable single-ingredient tablet concepts. Use BROAD mappings to `estetrol 14.2 MG` (36026937; 2539040) for 14.2 mg and `estetrol` (36026931; 2539031) for 18.9 mg. Suggestions record the full strength, oral-tablet form, and brand. These decisions agree with the existing EMA Fylrevy mappings in product folder 006213.
