# Granule presentations

[AEMPS Levetiracetam Desitin SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/74950/FT_74950.html) identifies coated granules in sachets. Use strength-specific Oral Granules concepts rather than Tablet for Oral Suspension.

[AEMPS Laurak SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/83242/FT_83242.html) identifies granules for oral solution. Standard RxNorm and Extension searches found strength-specific Oral Granules, but no clean strength-specific Granules for Oral Solution concepts. These are BROAD mappings with suggestions preserving the reconstitution form. The 1500 mg Extension granules concept preserves more information than the former ingredient-strength-only mapping.
