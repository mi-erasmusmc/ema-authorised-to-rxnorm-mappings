# Tiotropium strength conventions

Reviewed 2026-11-14. Use capsule content for RxNorm Inhalation Powder and delivered amount per actuation for Inhalation Spray.

- [Gregal SmPC, section 2](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/80980/FT_80980.html): capsule contains 13 micrograms tiotropium (16 micrograms bromide); 10 micrograms are delivered through the Zonda mouthpiece. Use clean Extension `tiotropium 0.013 MG Inhalation Powder` rather than the incorrect standard 18 microgram concept.
- [Braltus SmPC, section 2](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/80981/FichaTecnica_80981.html): same 13 microgram capsule content and 10 microgram delivered dose. Extension is needed for the capsule strength; the matching Braltus branded Extension concept is appropriate.
- [Spiriva Respimat SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/69589/FichaTecnica_69589.html): 2.5 micrograms tiotropium delivered per actuation; two actuations deliver 5 micrograms. Use the 0.0025 MG/ACTUAT concept, not 0.00125 MG/ACTUAT. The 2.5 mg entry in principios_activos for cod_nacion 725702 is a source unit error.
- [Sirkava leaflet](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/p/84261/P_84261.html): capsule contains 18 micrograms tiotropium, delivering 12 micrograms through NeumoHaler. The standard 0.018 MG Inhalation Powder concept matches exactly; the delivered-dose difference does not require a BROAD mapping.
