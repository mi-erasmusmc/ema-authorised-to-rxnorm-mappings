## Mapping review 2026-11-11

[Besponsa EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/besponsa-epar-product-information_en.pdf) identifies 1 mg inotuzumab ozogamicin per vial. Reconstitution with 4 mL yields 0.25 mg/mL for single use; unused solution is discarded. The marketed product is a powder, represented by its total vial strength and Injection form.

Standard RxNorm offers a 0.9 mg injection or a strength-unspecified branded injection. Hecate Extension search returned the clean branded 1 mg Injection concept 2040847 (OMOP4940516), which adds the clinically relevant matching strength. Selected EXACT; excluded supplier-specific and multidose Injectable Solution candidates. This agrees with the reviewed EMA mapping for EU/1/17/1200/001.
