# Teicoplanin presentation mapping

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [AEMPS: Targocid 200 mg and 400 mg, sections 2 and 6.6](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/58921/FichaTecnica_58921.html): each vial contains 200 mg or 400 mg; reconstitution yields the corresponding dose in 3 mL. The medicine is for single use. The supplied solvent volume and empty vial capacity are not the administered dose volume.
- [AEMPS: Teicoplanina Sala 200 mg and 400 mg](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/78750/FichaTecnica_78750.html): confirms labeled powder-vial strengths and reconstituted doses of 200 mg or 400 mg in 3 mL. The reconstituted presentation supports parenteral use and can also be administered orally for the specified indication; rows listing oral administration still represent the same injectable powder vial.

Hecate searches of standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension found standard concentration-based Injectable Solution concepts (66.7 and 133 MG/ML), but Extension provides clean dose-based Injection concepts. Injection matches the single-use reconstituted powder presentation and retains the labeled vial dose without relying on rounded reconstitution concentrations. Use 21172842 / OMOP408148 for generic 200 mg vials and 21025554 / OMOP408135 for generic 400 mg vials. For Targocid, use 35829424 / OMOP5038607 and 2912612 / OMOP5129389 respectively. Extension is required for the clinically appropriate single-use dose form; the matching brand is retained where available.
