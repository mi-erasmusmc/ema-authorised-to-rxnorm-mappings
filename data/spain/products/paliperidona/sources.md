Mapping evidence for the Cinfa prefilled syringes (cod_nacion 768698, 768699, 768700 and 768702):

- [AEMPS Paliperidona Cinfa SmPC, registration 90629](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/90629/FT_90629.html), section 2: syringe strengths are expressed as paliperidone base equivalent, supplied as paliperidone palmitate.
- The repository's `weight_conversions.tsv` records 50/75/100/150 mg paliperidone as 78/117/156/234 mg paliperidone palmitate. Combined with the respective 0.5/0.75/1/1.5 mL volumes, each maps to the corresponding unbranded 156 MG/ML palmitate prefilled syringe concept.
