# Formulation review

[Salofalk 1000 mg SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/140/smpc) describes gastro-resistant granules with pH-dependent release from the coating and prolonged release from the matrix. The 1000 mg delayed-release oral granules Extension concept is more specific than plain oral granules.

[Pentasa Sachet SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/1291/smpc) describes prolonged-release ethylcellulose-coated microgranules. Do not transfer the Salofalk enteric-release mapping to Pentasa granules merely because the strength matches.

Pentasa 1000 mg tablets use the matching extended-release tablet concept. Claversal 1000 mg gastro-resistant tablets use the delayed-release tablet concept. Existing Mezavant brand concepts remain valid; the equivalent standard unbranded concept is also valid and is retained where already used, consistent with the rule against upgrades solely for Extension brand detail.
