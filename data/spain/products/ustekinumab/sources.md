# Ustekinumab mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-06.

## Biosimilar identity

The shared biosimilar reference and the mappings in this folder use the following verified EU-to-US links:

- **Uzpruvo → ustekinumab-aekn.** The [EMA assessment report](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/uzpruvo-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf) identifies Uzpruvo as AVT04. The [FDA review](https://www.fda.gov/media/179791/download) identifies AVT04 as Selarsdi, ustekinumab-aekn. [Alvotech's market-entry announcement](https://www.alvotech.com/newsroom/alvotech-announces-expected-global-market-entry-dates-for-avt04-biosimilar-to-stelara-ustekinumab) also connects these products. Use unbranded suffixed concepts for the two syringe strengths and the 26 mL infusion vial.
- **Usymro → ustekinumab-hmny.** The [EMA assessment report](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/assessment-report/usymro-epar-public-assessment-report_en.pdf), section 2.4.4, identifies Usymro as BAT2206. [Hikma's FDA approval announcement](https://www.hikma.com/news/bio-thera-solutions-and-hikma-pharmaceuticals-announce-fda-approval-of-starjemza-ustekinumab-hmny-injection-a-biosimilar-referencing-stelara-ustekinumab-injection/) identifies Starjemza (ustekinumab-hmny) as BAT2206. Use unbranded suffixed concepts for the two syringe strengths and the 26 mL infusion vial.

These are identity links through the development product, not deductions merely from sharing Stelara as reference product. They do not assert regulatory interchangeability across jurisdictions.

## Device and vial distinctions

Hecate standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension searches found exact Pyzchiva auto-injectors, but no matching auto-injectors for Stelara, Wezenla, Otulfi, Yesintek, or Steqeyma. Searches included suffixed ingredient names and EU brands. Retain those ten pen rows as BROAD with auto-injector suggestions. For biosimilar pens, use matching-volume suffixed prefilled-syringe concepts to preserve biosimilar identity and dose; the device mismatch remains explicit. For Stelara pens, retain matching-volume branded Injection concepts.

Pyzchiva 45 mg vial (768379) is 0.5 mL at 90 mg/mL: use the available ustekinumab-ttwe Injection concept, not Prefilled Syringe. The source now gives the strength and volume explicitly.

Where structured form fields conflict, the product name, clinical presentation, container unit and route support the existing mapping: Otulfi 130 mg is an IV infusion vial despite a syringe form field; Usymro syringes are subcutaneous despite an infusion form field. Otulfi pen 770088 has a syringe container label but the name, clinical presentation, form and container unit all identify a pen.
