# Glucose mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-11.

## Clinical strengths

- [AEMPS Glucosa Physan 5% SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/pdfs/es/ft/67795/67795_ft.pdf), [KabiPac 5% SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/58903/FT_58903.html), and [Freeflex 5% SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/45441/FT_45441.html): 5 g anhydrous glucose per 100 mL, supplied as 5.5 g monohydrate. Map the labeled 50 mg/mL clinical strength, not the inherited 56.5 mg/mL.
- [AEMPS Viaflex 20% SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/53141/FT_53141.html): 200 mg/mL anhydrous glucose (220 mg/mL monohydrate). Use the labeled clinical strength of 200 mg/mL.
- [Archived manufacturer SmPC for Solucion Glucosada 4.8%, hosted by the Spanish pharmacists council](https://botplusweb.farmaceuticos.com/documentos/2009/1/28/37297.pdf): 4.8 g anhydrous glucose per 100 mL, supplied as 5.3 g monohydrate. The 48 mg/mL strength must not be mapped EXACT to 50 mg/mL.
- [AEMPS Glucocemin 33% SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/74778/FT_74778.html): 330 mg/mL anhydrous glucose (363 mg/mL monohydrate), in 10 mL ampoules. The inherited 333 mg/mL concept is not the labeled strength.
- [AEMPS Gluintol 50 g SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/79830/FT_79830.html): 25 g glucose monohydrate per 100 mL, with 200 mL per bottle. Use a suggestion of 250 mg/mL Oral Solution, preserving the labeled monohydrate concentration; the source des_dcp 5% and inherited 50 mg/mL mapping are inconsistent with the SmPC.

## Single-use infusion presentations

KabiPac and Freeflex SmPC section 6.6 specifies that each container is for one infusion and unused solution is discarded. Viaflex section 6.6 also specifies single use, disposal of partially used bags, and no reconnection. These infusion presentations are represented with Injection, preserving the volume in des_dcp rather than classifying the containers as multidose Injectable Solution.

Standard RxNorm volume-specific Injection concepts are preferred. The 1000 mL, 200 mg/mL infusion and 20 mL, 500 mg/mL ampoules require clean RxNorm Extension Injection concepts. Where standard and Extension searches do not find the exact volume, the mapping is BROAD to a concentration-level Injection with the full volume-specific suggestion. Where 48 or 330 mg/mL is unavailable, glucose Injection is used BROAD without substituting a nearby strength. Gluintol uses glucose Oral Solution BROAD because no 250 mg/mL oral solution was found.
