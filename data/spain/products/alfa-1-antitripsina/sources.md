# Alpha-1-antitrypsin mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-13.

[DailyMed PROLASTIN-C](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=91edab72-c889-470e-8315-1798b5548dca) links the plasma-derived powder for solution to RxCUI 1661208, `alpha 1-proteinase inhibitor, human 1 MG Injection`. The 1 MG is a normalized dosing unit, not the nominal vial potency. Use that standard clinical concept; retain actual 500/1000/4000/5000 MG vial strengths in source data. Existing Respreeza mappings use the same convention.

[CIMA Prolastina 4000/5000 MG SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/89084/FT_89084.html) and [CIMA Prolasplan 4000 MG SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/90820/FT_90820.html) describe powders reconstituted into solutions for infusion. The inherited Extension Injectable Suspension concepts are inappropriate. The standard normalized Injection concept represents a reconstituted solution and avoids that form error.

The Spanish brands differ from the US Prolastin/Zemaira brands, so the mappings remain unbranded. Strength-specific Extension results were reviewed, but do not supersede an adequate standard RxNorm dosing-unit concept.
