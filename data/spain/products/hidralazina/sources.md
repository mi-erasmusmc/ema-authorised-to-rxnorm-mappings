# Mapping sources

- [AEMPS Hydrapres 20 mg SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/56236/): sections 2, 4.2 and 6.6 specify 20 mg per powder ampoule, reconstitution with 1.1 mL water, a labeled concentration of 20 mg/mL, and a maximum administered volume of 1 mL. The mapping uses the 1 mL, 20 mg/mL single-use Injection concept.
