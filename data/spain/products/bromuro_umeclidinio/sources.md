# Dose convention

[AEMPS Incruse Ellipta SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/114922002/FT_114922002.html): the 55 microgram delivered umeclidinium dose corresponds to a 62.5 microgram pre-dispensed dose. RxNorm uses the latter convention (0.0625 MG/ACTUAT). The source presentations contain 30 actuations.
