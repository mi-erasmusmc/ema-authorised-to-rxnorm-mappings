## Mapping review 2026-11-11

[AEMPS SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/71492/FT_71492.html), sections 2, 4.2, 5.1 and 6.5: iodinated norcholesterol is 6-(I-131) iodomethyl norcholesterol, 7.5-15 MBq/mL at calibration, supplied in a multidose vial. The 15 mL figure is vial capacity; actual fill is 2.4-10 mL.

Hecate standard and Extension searches found isotope-specific ingredient NORCHOLESTENOL IODOMETHYL I-131, concept 36858005 / OMOP5175620. This replaces elemental iodine, preserving both molecular identity and isotope. No matching activity-concentration/dose-form concept found, so BROAD. Suggestion uses Injectable Solution for the multidose form, without packaging volume. Using 1 mCi = 37 MBq, 7.5-15 MBq/mL = 0.2027027-0.4054054 mCi/mL, rounded to six decimals.
