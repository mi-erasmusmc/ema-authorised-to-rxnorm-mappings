# Follitropin alfa mapping review

Reviewed 2026-11-13 against EMA product information and the current Hecate RxNorm/RxNorm Extension search results.

- [GONAL-f product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/gonal-f-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 6.5: the 1050 IU/1.75 mL presentation is a multidose vial whose reconstituted solution contains 600 IU/mL. Map EXACT to the branded concentration-level Injectable Solution; vial content is not a single dose. The 150 IU pen contains 0.24 mL, giving 625 IU/mL despite the rounded 0.25 mL CIMA clinical description. Its concentration-only branded pen concept remains BROAD because no matching 0.24 mL concept was found. Current RxNorm identifies concept 46234182 (1654175) as 625 UNT/ML, correcting the stale 600 UNT/ML name in the existing mapping. The 75 IU powder presentations have a matching branded Injection concept.
- [Ovaleap product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/ovaleap-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2, 4.2 and 5.1: biosimilar supplied as 0.5, 0.75 and 1.5 mL cartridges at 600 IU/mL, equivalent to 44 micrograms/mL (0.044 MG/ML), for subcutaneous injection with a separate pen. CIMA's infusion dose form is inconsistent with the SmPC. Replace inherited pen concepts, including the incorrect 625 UNT/ML presentations, with unbranded Extension Cartridge concepts. Keep BROAD with Ovaleap-specific suggestions because no FDA-suffixed counterpart was identified in terminology searches.
- [Bemfola product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/bemfola-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2: all five prefilled pen presentations contain 600 IU/mL, equivalent to 44 micrograms/mL. Existing BROAD mappings preserve the appropriate volume and concentration; retained after confirming the Extension concepts. No FDA-suffixed counterpart was identified in terminology searches.

Standard RxNorm was searched before Extension. Extension is needed to preserve Ovaleap's cartridge form and Bemfola's pen volumes/concentrations. Supplier-specific and box-count concepts were excluded. Biosimilar suggestions use the repository's placeholder suffix convention; they do not assert an assigned FDA name.
