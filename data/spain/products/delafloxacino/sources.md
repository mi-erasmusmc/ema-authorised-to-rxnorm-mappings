# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [EMA Quofenix product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/quofenix-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 6.6: the powder vial contains 300 mg delafloxacin as meglumine. It is reconstituted to 12 mL (25 MG/ML), then further diluted for IV infusion; unused reconstituted solution is discarded. Total-dose Injection is appropriate for this powder vial.
- Standard RxNorm search confirms delafloxacin 300 MG Injection (1592958; 1927667) and delafloxacin 450 MG Oral Tablet (1592968; 1927677). The vial mapping type is corrected to EXACT; the existing exact tablet mapping is retained. The US brand Baxdela is not used for Quofenix. Source principios_activos uses salt wording, but the labelled 300/450 mg strengths are delafloxacin base.
