# Urea (13C) mapping review

Reviewed 2026-11-11 using AEMPS product information and the Hecate RxNorm/RxNorm Extension search.

- [Helicobacter Test INFAI SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/97045001/FT_97045001.html): 75 mg carbon-13-labelled urea per jar, powder for oral solution. Reconstituted using approximately 30 ml water in total. The source's `vial para inyección` unit is container metadata, not the route. Use the clean matching branded Extension concept 40742919 (OMOP4714774), EXACT. Do not use ordinary urea, an injectable concept, a supplier-specific concept, or Oral Powder (which excludes powders for reconstitution).
- [UBTest SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/67650/fichatecnica_67650.html): 100 mg carbon-13-labelled urea in a film-coated tablet swallowed whole. Use unbranded Extension concept 42956296 (OMOP4682834), EXACT; UBIT is a different brand.
- [Espikur SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/84708/FT_84708.html): 50 mg carbon-13-labelled urea in a tablet swallowed whole. Retain Extension concept 40742951 (OMOP4714743), EXACT.
- [TAU-KIT SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/61805/FT_61805.html): soluble 100 mg tablet, dissolved in water before ingestion. Adults use 100 mg in 125 ml; children use 50 mg in 50 ml. Citral Pylori is administered separately as a test meal. No suitable soluble-tablet concept was found. Preserve isotope and strength with Extension component 42956298 (OMOP4682836), BROAD. The suggestion `urea c-13 100 MG Oral Tablet` expresses the supplied strength and general tablet form using the available RxNorm dose-form vocabulary; the missing soluble-tablet detail is recorded in the mapping comment. This is not a recommendation to swallow TAU-KIT whole. Do not use Disintegrating Oral Tablet, whose definition specifies dissolution in the mouth, or impose a single reconstituted concentration across adult and paediatric use.

Carbon-13 is a stable, non-radioactive isotope. Its identity is essential to these diagnostic products; ordinary urea is not an equivalent mapping. Standard RxNorm search returned other strengths/formulations (including 125 mg oral suspension and 1.67 mg/ml oral solution), which do not match these presentations. Extension is needed for the relevant strengths and forms.
