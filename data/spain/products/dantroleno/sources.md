# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [EMA Agilus product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/agilus-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 4.2: each vial contains 120 mg dantrolene sodium hemiheptahydrate. Adding 20 mL water gives a final volume of 22.6 mL at the labelled 5.3 MG/ML hydrate concentration. The previous dantrolene sodium 0.333 MG/ML mapping describes a different formulation.
- Standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension searches covered Agilus, 120 mg, hydrate names, 5.3 MG/ML and base-equivalent 94.24 mg. No suitable matching strength-specific clinical drug concept was returned. Extension dantrolene 120 MG Injectable Solution does not identify the hydrate basis and uses a multidose form; it was not accepted as a matching dose. Repository weight_conversions.tsv records 120 mg hydrate as 94.24 mg dantrolene base.
- Standard RxNorm dantrolene Injection (42629601; 1791243) retains ingredient and parenteral form without imposing an incorrect strength. BROAD; the suggestion uses the labelled hydrate strength for the powder vial.
