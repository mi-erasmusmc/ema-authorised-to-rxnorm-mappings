## Mapping review 2026-11-11

[Pulmotec AEMPS SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/63210/FT_63210.html) describes a 1.340 g high-purity graphite crucible kit. Sodium pertechnetate Tc-99m is added separately; heating under argon generates radiolabeled carbon aerosol. Graphite mass is not an administered radiopharmaceutical strength.

Hecate searches of RxNorm and Extension for Pulmotec, Technegas and technetium Tc-99m carbon found no suitable kit/aerosol concept. Elemental technetium omits both radiolabel and carbon formulation and is not contained in the supplied kit. Graphite ingredient alone would describe only the precursor, without the radiopharmaceutical preparation. Marked NO_MAPPING. The suggestion names the intended generated aerosol using the available Inhalation Spray form; exact kit/aerosol terminology is unavailable.

[US Technegas DailyMed label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=10320da4-0c64-4d03-e063-6394a90a1923) also lists this product as outside RxNorm scope. This supports the search result but does not exclude future Extension coverage.
