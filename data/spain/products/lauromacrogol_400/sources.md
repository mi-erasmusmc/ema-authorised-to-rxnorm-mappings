# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [AEMPS Etoxisclerol 30 MG/ML SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/55549/FT_55549.html): lauromacrogol 400 solution is supplied in 2 mL ampoules; the label describes the 5, 10, 20 and 30 MG/ML concentration range. Source data identify each requested presentation's strength and 2 mL ampoule.
- Lauromacrogol 400 is polidocanol, as reflected in the source ATC C05BB02 and RxNorm ingredient terminology. Hecate searches found exact standard RxNorm 2 ML Injection concepts for 5 and 10 MG/ML and exact RxNorm Extension 2 ML Injection concepts for 20 and 30 MG/ML. Multidose Injectable Solution concepts were not selected for these ampoules.
