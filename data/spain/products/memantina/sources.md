# Starter packs and brand handling

[AEMPS Memantina Stada starter-pack SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/78345/FT_78345.html) and [AEMPS Memantina Ratiopharm leaflet](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/p/113836023/P_113836023.html) specify 28 tablets: seven each at 5, 10, 15 and 20 mg. Use the count-specific Extension pack rather than the ingredient-strength combination pack without counts.

Existing matching Ebixa Extension brand concepts remain valid. The equivalent standard RxNorm unbranded 10 mg tablet is also valid and retained where already used; no Extension upgrade solely for branding is required. The corresponding consistency flags are documented exceptions.
