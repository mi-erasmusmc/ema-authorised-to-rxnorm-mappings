# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [AEMPS Hexvix SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/66925/FichaTecnica_66925.html), sections 2 and 4.2: 85 mg hexaminolevulinate (as hydrochloride) is reconstituted in 50 mL to 1.7 MG/ML, and the 50 mL solution is instilled into the bladder. The labelled strength is base, despite the salt wording in source principios_activos.
- Standard RxNorm search returned ingredient-level hexyl 5-aminolevulinate only. Extension searches found the existing concentration-and-brand concept but no suitable full intravesical presentation. Injectable Solution, Prefilled Syringe and Topical Powder candidates have unsuitable forms; Aminolevulinate Irrigation Solution candidates do not identify the hexyl ester. The existing BROAD concept is retained and its missing form is specified in the suggestion.
