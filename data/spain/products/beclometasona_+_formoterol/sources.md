# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-20.

- [Blocetta 100/6 SmPC, section 2](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/90840/FT_90840.html): each metered actuation contains 100 micrograms beclometasone dipropionate and 6 micrograms formoterol fumarate dihydrate. Delivered amounts are 84.6 and 5.0 micrograms respectively.
- [Blocetta 200/6 SmPC, section 2](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/90839/FT_90839.html): each metered actuation contains 200 micrograms beclometasone dipropionate and 6 micrograms formoterol fumarate dihydrate. Delivered amounts are 177.7 and 5.1 micrograms respectively.

Use the labeled metered strengths (0.1 or 0.2 MG/ACTUAT and 0.006 MG/ACTUAT) with the matching RxNorm Extension presentation concepts. Extension uses the ingredient labels Beclomethasone/formoterol for these European labeled strengths. Do not substitute delivered strengths or a different corticosteroid combination.

Hecate standard RxNorm searches found no suitable full combination; Extension searches returned strength-specific solution and dry-powder concepts. The legacy Extension term `Inhalant Solution` represents the metered pressurised solution in the selected per-actuation concepts. These mappings preserve the full ingredient combination, labeled strengths, and solution form; matching branded concepts are used when available. The existing exact 200/6 dry-powder mappings are retained.
