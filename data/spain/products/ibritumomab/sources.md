# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [AEMPS Zevalin SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/03264001/FT_03264001.html), section 2: the kit contains 3.2 mg ibritumomab tiuxetan in 2 mL (1.6 MG/ML), plus sodium acetate, buffer and an empty reaction vial. Yttrium-90 is not included. The final radiolabelled preparation has a different concentration (2.08 mg in 10 mL), so it must not be substituted for the supplied precursor.
- Standard RxNorm search returned ingredient-level ibritumomab tiuxetan. Extension searches returned clean 2 ML ibritumomab tiuxetan 1.6 MG/ML Injectable Solution concepts, including the matching Zevalin brand. The branded volume-specific concept is a more informative BROAD match than ingredient alone, preserving the precursor vial dose and volume. Its multidose form and omission of the full kit prevent EXACT. The suggestion represents the single-use precursor vial; it does not assert that yttrium-90 is supplied in the kit.
