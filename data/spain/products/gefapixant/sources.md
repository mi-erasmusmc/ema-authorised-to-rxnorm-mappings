# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [EMA Lyfnua](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/lyfnua) identifies gefapixant as the active substance. [Manufacturer SmPC](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/15778/smpc), sections 2 and 3: each film-coated tablet contains gefapixant citrate equivalent to 45 mg gefapixant.
- Hecate standard RxNorm and Extension searches covered gefapixant, Lyfnua, and the 45 mg tablet. Only the existing standard ingredient concept GEFAPIXANT (36853165; OMOP5170780) was suitable. It is retained as BROAD with a complete strength/form/brand suggestion.
