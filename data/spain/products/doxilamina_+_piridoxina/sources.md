# Modified-release capsules

[AEMPS Cariban SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/44139/FichaTecnica_44139.html) specifies a hard modified-release capsule containing 10 mg doxylamine succinate and 10 mg pyridoxine hydrochloride. The Extension delayed-release capsule matches the Spanish labeled clinical strengths and capsule form; the standard delayed-release tablet has the wrong solid dose form. Navalem has the same capsule presentation in the source data.
