# Thiotepa mapping rationale

Reviewed 2026-11-06 using Hecate standard RxNorm searches for thiotepa and
Tepadina at 15, 100, and 400 mg.

Standard RxNorm has matching branded Injection concepts for all three Tepadina
strengths: 1594020 (1919211), 1594018 (1919209), and 1316679 (2734126), respectively.
Use these EXACT for Tepadina; retain the existing unbranded EXACT 15 mg and 100 mg
Injection concepts for the other brands/generics.

The [Spanish Tepadina 400 mg SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/110622003/FT_110622003.html)
confirms a 400 mg powder and solvent presentation in a dual-chamber bag, yielding
400 mL at 1 mg/mL after reconstitution. The source data incorrectly describes the
container as a vial. The [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/tepadina-epar-product-information_en.pdf)
confirms single use. The 400 mg Injection concept represents the supplied powder
unit strength; it is not a concentration-only mapping of the reconstituted liquid.
