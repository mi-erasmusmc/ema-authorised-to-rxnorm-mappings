
## Relvar Ellipta dose convention (2026-11-09)

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/relvar-ellipta-epar-product-information_en.pdf), section 2, identifies delivered 92/22 micrograms as corresponding to pre-dispensed 100/25 micrograms. The two new 30-dose presentations therefore match the existing 30 ACTUAT unbranded clinical drug concept.
