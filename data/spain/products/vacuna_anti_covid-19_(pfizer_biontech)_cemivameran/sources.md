# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [AEMPS 3 microgram SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1201528042/FT_1201528042.html): after dilution, 3 micrograms per 0.3 mL = 0.01 MG/ML. The existing strain-specific Injectable Suspension concept is retained as EXACT.
- [AEMPS 10 microgram SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1201528041/FT_1201528041.html) and [30 microgram SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1201528038/FT_1201528038.html): multidose vials deliver six 0.3 mL doses. Concentrations are 0.0333 and 0.1 MG/ML respectively. The available strain-specific concepts use single-use Injection, so these two mappings are BROAD with suggestions for Injectable Suspension. The 30 microgram concept uses the matching Comirnaty 2024-2025 brand.
- Hecate standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension searches covered named INNs, KP.2/XBB.1.5 strains, concentration tiers and multidose Injectable Suspension forms.
