# Mapping evidence

- [AEMPS Reflex Forte leaflet](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/p/59479/P_59479.html), checked 2026-11-10: each gram of gel contains methyl salicylate 100 mg, turpentine oil 60 mg, camphor 30 mg and menthol 30 mg.
- Hecate RxNorm and Extension searches, 2026-11-10: no full four-ingredient gel found. Standard concept `1511334` matches the gel form and the strengths of camphor, menthol and methyl salicylate. Map BROAD, explicitly recording omitted turpentine in the comment and full suggestion.
- Reflex Spray source data specifies camphor 40 mg/ml, menthol 40 mg/ml, methyl salicylate 25 mg/ml and turpentine 65 mg/ml. Extension strength-specific spray concepts found use methyl salicylate 27 mg/ml and turpentine 67.4 mg/ml, so these are not exact matches. Concept `36267047` preserves all four ingredients, spray form and matching Reflexspray brand, with BROAD type and the source strengths in the suggestion.
