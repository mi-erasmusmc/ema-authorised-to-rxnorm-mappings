# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [AEMPS Termalgin Resfriado SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/78433/FT_78433.html), sections 2 and 3: each sachet contains 500 mg paracetamol and 30 mg pseudoephedrine hydrochloride as powder for oral solution. Paracetamol is acetaminophen in RxNorm.
- Standard RxNorm and Extension searches did not return an exact two-ingredient 500/30 mg Powder for Oral Solution concept. The existing standard RxNorm tablet concept preserves both ingredients and doses and remains BROAD for its form mismatch. Candidates with 60 mg pseudoephedrine or extra active ingredients were not selected. The suggestion records the labelled sachet presentation.
