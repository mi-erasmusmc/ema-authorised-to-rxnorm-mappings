- [AEMPS Algesal Activado ointment SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/49339/FT_49339.html): 100 mg diethylamine salicylate and 10 mg myrtecaine per gram of ointment.
- Both confirm the product-name strengths rather than the inconsistent 400 mg/g value in data.tsv des_dosific. Foam maps BROAD to the strength-matched ointment; the suggestion retains the foam form and laurilsulfate salt.
