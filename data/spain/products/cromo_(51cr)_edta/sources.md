Reviewed 2026-11-11.

The repository source identifies chromium-51 edetate at 3.7 MBq/mL, intravenous injectable solution. The [original CIMA SmPC URL](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/75246/FT_75246.html) was unavailable during review; no additional container-volume assumption was made.

Standard RxNorm searches did not identify the edetate radiopharmaceutical. Extension provides CHROMIUM CR-51 EDETATE, concept 36859702 (OMOP5177318), which preserves isotope and chemical complex and improves on plain chromium. Map BROAD because activity concentration and dose form are absent.

Extension also returned CHROMIUM CR-51 0.1 MCI/ML Injectable Solution (42935509) and a Korean branded EDTA solution. Although 0.1 mCi/mL equals 3.7 MBq/mL, the unbranded concept does not explicitly identify edetate, and the Korean brand does not match. The explicit edetate ingredient is the conservative choice.
