## Mapping review 2026-11-11

[Hetronifly AEMPS SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1241870001/FT_1241870001.html), sections 2, 4.2, 6.5 and 6.6: 10 mg/mL serplulimab concentrate for intravenous infusion; each 10 mL vial contains 100 mg. The required volume is withdrawn and diluted before infusion. The suggestion represents the supplied vial concentration and volume, not the final diluted infusion concentration.

Hecate searches of standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension used serplulimab, Hetronifly, 10 mg/mL, Injection, Injectable Solution and the 10 mL presentation. Only the exact serplulimab ingredient was found: 36861273 / OMOP5178890. Retained BROAD. Both Spain and EMA suggestions use 10 ML serplulimab 10 MG/ML Injection [Hetronifly]. The earlier EMA wording, Concentrate for Injectable Solution, is not a current normalized RxNorm dose form. EMA parsed data confirms the same 10 mg/mL concentrate, 10 mL vial and intravenous route; Spain nro_definitivo 1241870001 corresponds to that EMA authorisation. Similar names and other anti-PD-1 antibodies returned by semantic search are different ingredients and were excluded.

[EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/hetronifly-epar-product-information_en.pdf), Annex III carton labeling, states single use only. [RxNorm dose-form definitions](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/research/umls/rxnorm/docs/appendix2.html) use Injection for single-use parenteral solutions and Injectable Solution for multidose products; dilution before infusion does not require a separate concentrate dose form.
