# Ranibizumab mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-06.

## Delivered dose and overfill

The [EMA Ranluspec product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/ranluspec-epar-product-information_en.pdf), sections 2 and 4.2, distinguishes the 0.23 mL vial and 0.165 mL prefilled syringe from the administered 0.05 mL dose (0.5 mg at 10 mg/mL). Excess solution is discarded before administration. The [EMA Byooviz information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/byooviz-epar-product-information_en.pdf) likewise describes a 0.165 mL prefilled syringe delivering 0.05 mL. The [EMA Rimmyrah information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/rimmyrah-epar-product-information_en.pdf) describes a 0.23 mL vial providing a 0.05 mL dose.

Use the standard RxNorm 0.05 mL clinical-drug convention for these intravitreal presentations. Retain the existing Lucentis and Ranivisio EXACT mappings, whose comments already identify the delivered dose after overfill removal. Do not reinterpret the whole container contents as the administered dose or change the 10 mg/mL concentration.

## Recent biosimilar approvals

- **Ranluspec:** The [FDA Purple Book, BLA 761480](https://purplebooksearch.fda.gov/index.cfm?blaNo=761480&event=productdetails) identifies ranibizumab-hkdz, approved 2 June 2026, including 10 mg/mL single-dose vial and prefilled-syringe presentations. The [FDA biosimilar list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/biosimilars/biosimilar-product-information) agrees on the suffix. The original FDA label contains a conflicting hdkz spelling; use hkdz as given by the Purple Book and FDA list. Both jurisdictions use the Ranluspec brand with Lupin as sponsor.
- **Ximluci → Lucamzi:** The [FDA biosimilar list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/biosimilars/biosimilar-product-information) records Lucamzi (ranibizumab-blrz), approved October 2026. [Xbrane's approval announcement](https://www.xbrane.com/en/fda-approves-lucamzi-biosimilar-to-lucentis/) connects the US product to Ximluci/XSB-001. Replace the placeholder suffix in Ximluci's suggestion with blrz.
- **Byooviz:** Retain its established ranibizumab-nuna identity from the shared biosimilar reference.
- **Rimmyrah:** Retain the existing BROAD plain-INN mapping and placeholder suffix; no FDA counterpart is documented in the shared reference.

## Concept coverage

Standard RxNorm and Extension searches found no matching ranibizumab-hkdz (also searched as hdkz), ranibizumab-blrz, or ranibizumab-nuna Prefilled Syringe concepts. This is a search finding, not a claim about every terminology release.

Ranluspec vial and syringe therefore map BROAD to matching-strength, matching-form plain ranibizumab concepts, with hkdz suggestions. Ximluci remains BROAD with a blrz suggestion. Byooviz syringe maps BROAD to the unbranded 0.05 mL ranibizumab-nuna Injection concept, preserving identity and delivered dose while its suggestion records the missing syringe form. The shared reference now includes the two recent approvals.
