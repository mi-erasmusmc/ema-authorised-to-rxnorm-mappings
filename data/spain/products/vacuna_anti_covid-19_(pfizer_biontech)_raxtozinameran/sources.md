# Mapping evidence

Reviewed 2026-11-09.

- [AEMPS 3 microgram SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1201528024/FT_1201528024.html): the multidose concentrate delivers ten 0.2 mL doses after dilution, each containing 3 micrograms of raxtozinameran. The resulting concentration is 0.015 MG/ML, not the 0.01 MG/ML in the previous mapping. No suitable XBB.1.5 0.015 MG/ML concept was returned; the concentration-specific Pfizer mRNA Injectable Suspension fallback is BROAD because XBB.1.5 is not represented in that concept.
- [AEMPS 10 microgram SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1201528023/FT_1201528023.html) and [30 microgram SmPC](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/ft/1201528019/FT_1201528019.html), the [manufacturer SmPC authorisation table](https://webfiles.pfizer.com/SmPC_5-11_Ready_to_Use_Blue_XBB1.5): authorisations /023 and /019 identify multidose vials. Doses of 10 and 30 micrograms per 0.3 mL correspond to 0.0333 and 0.1 MG/ML. The available strain-specific concepts use single-use Injection, so these mappings are BROAD with multidose Injectable Suspension suggestions.
- Hecate standard RxNorm and RxNorm Extension searches covered named INNs, KP.2/XBB.1.5 strains, concentration tiers and multidose Injectable Suspension forms.
