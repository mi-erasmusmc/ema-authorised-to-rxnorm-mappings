# Tirzepatide mapping rationale

Reviewed 2026-11-06 using Hecate standard RxNorm searches for all six strengths
of the 2.4 mL Mounjaro Pen Injector.

All six Spanish presentations are multidose KwikPens containing four fixed
0.6 mL doses. The labeled mg/dose is not the total content of the 2.4 mL pen.
Standard RxNorm now provides matching branded Pen Injector concepts, so all six
map EXACT without requiring Extension or an Injectable Solution fallback.

| Dose | Total content | Concentration | Concept ID | RxNorm code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2.5 mg | 10 mg / 2.4 mL | 4.17 mg/mL | 1317524 | 2735286 |
| 5 mg | 20 mg / 2.4 mL | 8.33 mg/mL | 1317525 | 2735288 |
| 7.5 mg | 30 mg / 2.4 mL | 12.5 mg/mL | 1317526 | 2735290 |
| 10 mg | 40 mg / 2.4 mL | 16.7 mg/mL | 1317527 | 2735292 |
| 12.5 mg | 50 mg / 2.4 mL | 20.8 mg/mL | 1317528 | 2735294 |
| 15 mg | 60 mg / 2.4 mL | 25 mg/mL | 1317529 | 2735297 |

The [Spanish Mounjaro product information](https://cima.aemps.es/cima/dochtml/p/1221685051/Prospecto.html)
and [EMA product information](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/documents/product-information/mounjaro-epar-product-information_en.pdf)
describe these dose, volume, and concentration relationships. The
[US Mounjaro label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d2d7da5d-ad07-4228-955f-cf7e355c8cc0)
also describes the corresponding single-patient-use KwikPens.

The 12.5 mg/dose presentation specifically requires 20.8 mg/mL, not 20 mg/mL.
Do not substitute the 0.5 mL single-dose auto-injectors, which have different
concentrations and device characteristics.
